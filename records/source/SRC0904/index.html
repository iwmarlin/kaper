Evidence recorded in this source

Two entries in the same issue, and they are not the same kind of thing. On p. 99 the concert agency Impresariat Max Walther advertises the evening for Róża Etkin: “Bach-Saal, Sonnab., 3. Dezember, abends 8 Uhr Klavier-Abend F. Chopin (4 Balladen), Szymanowski, B. Kaper (Uraufführung). Ravel, Albeniz, Liszt (H-moll). Konzertflügel: Bechstein.” The word is the agency’s, in its own advertisement. On p. 92 the guide’s own index, headed “Erst- und Neuaufführungen in der Zeit vom 30. November bis 3. Dezember 1927”, lists “3. Dezember — Bachsaal — B. Kaper: Klavierkonzert” among five entries. That index does not separate a first performance from a new one, so it corroborates the date and the occasion but not the word Uraufführung, and its Klavierkonzert is the kind of concert rather than the title of a work: the column enters “Kodaly: Klavierkonzert” for 1 December at the Singakademie, and Kodály’s catalogue holds no piano concerto. The guide does keep its performance terms apart elsewhere on p. 99, marking Dohnányi’s third string quartet “(Urauff.)” and Shostakovich’s symphony “[Erstauff.]”. No source in the archive names the work Etkin played.
