Object context

The bird's-eye view shows two security booths, cars and pedestrians, an arcaded building on the Mexican side and a sign arching over the road reading “Mexico”, with further signs for Mexicali, Southern Club Cafe, Cafe Gabrinus and Climax Cafe. The negative sleeve is annotated “Mexican American Line, Calexico, Mexicali” and “California, Calexico-Mexicali”. UCLA suggests a possible connection to two Los Angeles Times reports from Calexico, “No Permit Needed at Mexican Border” of 6 July 1927 and “New Regulations in Force at Border” of 14 July 1927, but does not confirm publication.
