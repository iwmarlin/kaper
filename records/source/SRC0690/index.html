Evidence recorded in this source

The advertisement uses the pre-release title Der glückliche Herr Fünf, after Alice Berend’s novel Herr Fünf. It credits Heinz Hilpert as director, Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann with the music, Willi Domgraf-Fassbaender in the leading role, and Pax Film GmbH as producer. The released film is documented elsewhere as Ich will Dich Liebe lehren.
