Citation

Sirius-Mappen (36 Hefte). Wien–Berlin: Sirius-Verlag, 1927–January 1933. Six annual series. Digitised set with an indexed list of 278 titles, Internet Archive. Accessed 27 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Evidence recorded in this source

The indexed contents name eight Kaper titles, each with its series and issue number: “Auf Wiederseh’n, Herr Doktor! Auf Wiederseh’n, Frau Doktor!” (4-05), “Sagen kleine Mädels ‘nein’” (5-07), “Mondnacht in Sanssouci” (6-02), “Heut’ bin ich gut aufgelegt!”, “Ich weiß ein kleines Café” and “Es wird schon wieder besser” (all 6-09), “Wer weint heut’ aus Liebe Tränen” (6-10) and “Tränen in der Geige” (7-01). All eight are lyrics by Fritz Rotter. The last carries a series number beyond the six annual series of the Sirius-Mappen and probably belongs to the successor publication Tonfilm Theater Tanz, issued by the same house from 1933; it is listed here as the index gives it and has not been checked against the print.
