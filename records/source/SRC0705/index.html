Citation

Guy Berry. “Vivons l’amour, vivons la vie”, from the Casino de Paris revue Parade de France. Music-hall song by Walter Jurmann, Bronisław Kaper, Henri Varna and Marc Cab. Pathé PA362, matrix cpt-1514-1, 25 cm needle-cut electrical disc, 78 rpm, recorded October 1934. Phonobase record 15273; transfer made 4 February 2023 from the Christian Declerck collection, Dunkirk. Accessed 16 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Evidence recorded in this source

The Phonobase record additionally states that Tino Rossi created the song in 1934, that Mireille, Guy Berry and Priolet also performed it, and that Coda / Heugel published it in Paris in 1934.
