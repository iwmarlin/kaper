Citation

“Anläßlich eines Presseempfanges sprach der stellvertretende Produktionschef der Wien-Film Erich von Neusser über das Wiener Filmschaffen” [caption]. Photographs credited “Aufn. Wiener Bühne – Strof.” Die Bühne (Vienna), no. 6, 1940, p. 11. Österreichische Nationalbibliothek / ANNO.

Open source ↗
Object context

The page carries two photographs of the same occasion. The upper one shows von Neusser speaking, with the chief dramaturge Hans Gustl Kernmayr beside him; the lower one, from which the likeness here is taken, places him next to the actress Hilde Krahl and the director Gustav Ucicky. The caption states the office he then held, deputy head of production of Wien-Film, which no other record gathered for him gives, and dates it to 1940 — seven years after the Viennese film he directed to music by Kaper and Jurmann.
