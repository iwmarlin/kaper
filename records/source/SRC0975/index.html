Discographic note

The BnF dates the disc [1933] from the 1933 supplement of the Répertoire phonographique; it is a catalogue-supplied issue date, not a session date. The two matrix numbers are listed together, without a title-by-title assignment. The disc confirms that the French song was recorded under the film’s French title; its link to “Schade, daß Liebe ein Märchen ist” rests on the Salabert edition. The performers are not credited in the archive.
