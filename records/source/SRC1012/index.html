Date assessment

The Boston Public Library dates the poster to about 1920–1930. Sébille’s dates, 1874–1953, follow his authority records (BnF 147014081; VIAF 67528549). The poster shows the boat-train connection the French Line ran to its liners at Le Havre, by which Kaper sailed in 1934; it does not show the Champlain.
