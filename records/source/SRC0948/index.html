Date assessment

The report gives 6 September 1915 as the opening of the school year at the Staszic school and records twelve new pupils admitted to the fifth class, without naming them. The date is the school’s own and is later than the term announced generally for 4 September. The issue number and page have not been fixed here: the index of the 1917 volume lists an article under the title “Gimnazyum im. Staszica”, which may or may not be this report, and the reading of the report is the author’s rather than a second one made here.
