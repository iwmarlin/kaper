Verification note

USC gives 1935 as both creation and issue year. The available PDF contains the front cover and a publisher’s advertisement, not the musical notation of ‘Cosi Cosa’. The advertisement lists ‘San Francisco’ by Kahn, Kaper and Jurmann, a song registered in 1936; this raises a question about the date of the digitised copy or the relationship of its pages. The 1935 date is therefore retained as USC’s dating but qualified as uncertain for this copy, without assigning a new printing year. Neither available page nor the USC description names the film; the film connection rests on separate sources.
