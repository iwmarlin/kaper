Citation

Object context

The walk explains why this address can still be seen at all: Wilcza was bombed less heavily than the neighbouring streets, escaped the post-war plans for rebuilding the district, and kept a long row of its nineteenth- and early twentieth-century tenements, among them the houses at 25, 28, 30 and 32. The article documents the neighbouring 29A as the work of the practice of Wacław Heppen and Józef Napoleon Czerwiński, and its photograph shows that the roof turret of number 29 survives. What it does not give is a view of the house as Kaper knew it: the picture was taken in 2017 by the author of the article, and the street lost part of its fabric in 1944.
