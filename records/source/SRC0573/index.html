Lützowstraße 76, Berlin, with the Blüthner-Saal entrance — printed notice, 1912

Image or photograph

Citation

Halftone photograph of the façade of Lützowstraße 76, Berlin, with the entrance to the Blüthner-Saal and Klindworth-Scharwenka-Saal, printed above a notice headed “Oskar Schwalm’s Piano-Magazin” announcing that the salesroom for Blüthner pianos had recently moved into the building. Reproduced by GrammophonTeam in the thread “Bachsaal und Schwechtensaal – Lützowstrasse” on grammophon-platten.de (post of 15 July 2016) and dated there to 1912. No photographer and no place of first publication are given.
