Identity assessment

The photograph belongs to “Jazz und die Wiener Komponisten”, a page of statements by Viennese authors published for Paul Whiteman’s visit to the city, and is credited with the page’s other picture to the Willinger studio. The printed caption reads “Von links nach rechts: Dr. Robert Katscher, Armin Robinson, Paul Whiteman, Direktor Erwin Hein, Fritz Wreede, Direktor Otto Hein”, and six men are visible. Whiteman is certain: the same page prints him again, named, beside Katscher at a piano. Counting from the left he is third, which agrees with the caption and would make the second figure — balding, in round spectacles — Armin Robinson, then twenty-six years old. A portrait published elsewhere as Robinson shows a man with full dark hair and no spectacles, so the archive does not say which face here is his. The page carries the printed number 12; ANNO serves it as page 14, which is the address the link uses.
