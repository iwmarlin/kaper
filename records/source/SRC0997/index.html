Evidence recorded in this source

“En Sang for Dig” is the Danish title of Ein Lied für Dich. The Odeon disc D 609 by Bunds Danseorkester, with Valdemar Davids, gives the original title as “Ninon, lach mir einmal zu” (alma99122333533805763); Erik Tuxen’s orchestra recorded the song for Polyphon (X.S 50292) in 1933.
