Citation

Evidence recorded in this source

Entry no. 22781 records “The Orchestra in Love”: words and music by Fritz Rotter and B. Kaper, English words by H. Hailey-Simpson, of Great Britain; copyright 18 September 1931, one copy received 21 September 1931, E for. 20550, Chappell & Co. Ltd., London. Entry no. 27700 records “The Orchestra in Love” as a romantic-orchestra fox-trot intermezzo: words and music by Fritz Rotter and B. Kaper, English words by H. Hailey-Simpson, of Great Britain, 4to; copyright 16 October 1931, one copy received 29 October 1931, E for. 20990, Chappell & Co. Ltd., London.
