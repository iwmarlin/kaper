Walter Jurmann Collection finding aid — The Magic of Springtime in the Gus Kahn Papers

Archival manuscript holding

Citation

Finding Aid for the Walter Jurmann Collection, 1910–1988, Collection APAM-1. UCLA Library, Performing Arts Special Collections, Los Angeles; processed by Juliane Brand, 1989. Scope and Content note: the Papers of Gus Kahn in the Archive of Popular American Music include “an ozalid copy of a presumably unpublished Jurmann/Kaper/Kahn song, ‘The Magic of Springtime’, originally written for but never used in the 1936 MGM film ‘Maytime’”.

Date assessment

The cited passage was checked in a copy of the earlier APAM-1 finding aid. The current online finding aid for the Gus Kahn Papers describes film music for Maytime but does not identify this song by title; it is linked as collection context, not as the source of the quoted passage. The earlier finding aid describes Maytime as a 1936 film. The AFI Catalog dates its release to 26 March 1937 and records production in 1936–1937; the earlier wording is retained in the citation.
