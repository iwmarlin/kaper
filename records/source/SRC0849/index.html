Identity assessment

The note on the 1930 Homocord record sets out his working life: a singer heard on hundreds of records from 1928 until his death in 1945 during a tour entertaining the troops, often paired with the dance orchestra of Fred Bird, in private life Felix Lehmann. It also names two recording pseudonyms, Fred Lustig, under which he sings on the Odeon disc gathered here, and Ludwig Bernhuber, which no other record consulted for him gives. The portrait on the facing page is captioned with his name alone and is not dated by the publication; it is placed about 1930 from the record beside which it is printed.
