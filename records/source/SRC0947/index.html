Evidence recorded in this source

The article states that in July 1915 the Russian state and private secondary schools of Warsaw were evacuated into Russia together with their teaching staff, most of their pupils and their classroom equipment, and that the German army took over the school buildings on entering the city on 5 August. It is cited here for why the transfer from Skrynnikov’s private school to the Staszic Real School carries no day: the school year it belongs to was rebuilt from nothing through the autumn of 1915.
