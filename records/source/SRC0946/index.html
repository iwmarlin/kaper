Verification note

The ordinance is the measure that redefined a “German film” by the citizenship and origin of everyone working on a production, which is what closed the German market to Kaper and Jurmann as Jewish citizens of Poland and Austria. The text reaches this record through Loacker and Prucha rather than from the Reichsgesetzblatt, so the article is what the link resolves to and the reliability is set accordingly. The date is the date of the ordinance itself.
