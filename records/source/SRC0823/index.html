Citation

Société des auteurs, compositeurs et éditeurs de musique. “Henri Varna.” Musée SACEM, Les créateurs. Author, born in Marseille on 31 October 1887, died in Paris on 10 April 1969; elected full member of SACEM on 30 August 1918. Accessed 25 August 2026.

Authority note

The membership register places his admission to the society in 1918, nine years before he took over the direction of the Casino de Paris, and gives his birth and death to the day.
