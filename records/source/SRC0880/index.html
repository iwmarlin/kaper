Evidence recorded in this source

The finding aid lists the four songs of the Melodie der Liebe folio and gives the fourth as “Kinderlied (Es war einmal ein Baby--)”; its title index cross-refers “Kinderlied (Es war einmal ein Baby--)” to Melodie der Liebe.
