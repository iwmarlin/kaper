Citation

Hans Albers, with the Odeon-Künstler-Orchester under Otto Dobrindt. “Mein Gorilla hat 'ne Villa im Zoo.” Paso doble; Odeon O-11811, matrix Be 10227-2; recorded in Berlin, studio 2, on 13 February 1933 and released in April 1933. The uploader's description gives the matrix number and the label is not shown in the upload; the take, session date and release date follow the Odeon label list at musiktiteldb.de, which draws on the Discographie der deutschen Kleinkunst. The companion side, Odeon O-11811 with matrix Be 10226, survives with its label filmed and was recorded in the same session.
