Citation

Marek Weber und sein Orchester. “Du lieber Geiger, spiel’ einen Tango für mich.” Electrola E.G. 2271, matrix OD267-1, recorded in Berlin on 24 March 1931 and released June 1931. Digital transfer published on YouTube by 78rpm Musik on 27 April 2025; discographic data from the Electrola label list at musiktiteldb.de. Accessed 17 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Discographic note

The Electrola label list, drawing on the Discographie der deutschen Tanzmusik, describes the side as instrumental. This conflicts with the transfer description, which calls it a dance-orchestra recording with refrain vocal. The label is not shown in the upload.
