Identity assessment

The article names the pseudonym: Jurmann and Kaper had already had to leave Germany in 1933, “ebenso wie der Textdichter Fritz Rotter, der in diesem Fall ausnahmsweise einmal unter dem Pseudonym Peter Kuckuck firmierte” — as did the lyricist Fritz Rotter, who in this one case signed under the pseudonym Peter Kuckuck. It is a house history of the publisher that holds the surviving salon-orchestra edition of the song, photocopied in the late 1980s, and it names no document for the identification.
