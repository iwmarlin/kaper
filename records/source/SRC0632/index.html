Identity assessment

The BnF records a male composer and lyricist, born at an undetermined date in the nineteenth century and deceased in 1961, from SACEM information received in 1980; rejected forms are “Charline, Paul (18..-1961)”, a pseudonym, and “Hérembault, André-Paul Roubier d'”. This remains only a candidate identification for André Roubier: the uncommon surname, period and professional milieu agree, but BnF's single linked bibliographic record, the 1937 film song “Un carnet de bal / Valse grise”, credits him as lyricist under the name Paul Charline, whereas the Kaper-related records identify André Roubier as a composer. The BnF disc notice for “À Paris, tiguidiguidi” names neither Roubier nor any composer, crediting only Henri Garat as singer and Bronisław Kaper as conductor. No identity resolution is asserted.
