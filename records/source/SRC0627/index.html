Date assessment

The Library of Congress heading reads “Mann, Paul, 1910-1983” and cites the 1944 sheet “Tell me I'm the only one you care for”. The day and month of birth are contested: the German Wikipedia article gives 3 September 1910 while the GND gives 3 October 1910; both agree on the year and on the death date, 27 May 1983.
