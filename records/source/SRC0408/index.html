Evidence recorded in this source

The advertisement presents the film under the Mondial-Film name and explicitly credits Franz Wenzler as director, Fritz Rotter for the song lyrics, and Dr. B. Kaper and Walter Jurmann for the music. ‘Mondial-Film bringt’ documents the Austrian presentation or distribution context; it is not treated as a production-company credit.
