Discographic note

The upload description names Hans Albers with the Odeon-Künstler-Orchester under Otto Dobrindt but gives no catalogue or matrix number. Its authorship allocation—music to Jurmann and words to Kaper and Rotter—conflicts with the printed sources and is not used as authority for the work credits.
