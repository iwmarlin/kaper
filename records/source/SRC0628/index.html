Identity assessment

The French Wikipedia article gives the birth name as Marcel Robert Péguy and locates both his birth and death in the 17th arrondissement of Paris. Neither authority file records the spelling Pégy; the GND links directly to the filmportal.de record for the same man. The form printed in the credits of Son altesse l'amour is therefore treated as a source-specific spelling rather than as a name he is known to have used elsewhere.
