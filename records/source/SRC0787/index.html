Citation

“Wenn wir nicht beide in Gesellschaft wären, würde ich dir Liebe schwören (Vergißmeinnicht)”, melody by Bronislaw Kaper, words by Fritz Rotter, 1929. Indexed from 1000 Takte-Tanz, Band 2: 22 der populärsten Tanz- und Gesangs-Schlager, shelfmark A1184-2, page 12, with text, melody and piano setting. Deutsches Lied, index of German song collections, record 2197024.

Evidence recorded in this source

The index gives the melody to Kaper alone. Neither it nor the Jurmann work catalogue records a share for Walter Jurmann, whose credit was carried here without an item-level source and has been withdrawn; that catalogue does not list the song at all, and no longer stands among the sources for it.
