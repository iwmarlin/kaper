Authority note

The BnF records “Steinhof, Ninon” as a pseudonym and international form, identifies her as a woman writing in French and translating from German, and describes her as a translator. It gives the dates as “Naissance : 18..?” and “Mort : 19..”, indicating a nineteenth-century birth and a twentieth-century death, with neither year established; “Steinhoff, Ninon” is a rejected form. The notice cites two sources: the printed music “Adieu”, music by Pierre Candel, words by Ninon Steinhoff and André Legrand, 1934; and the French adaptation of Ferdinand Bruckner's play “Les criminels”, made with André Mauprey in 1950. ISNI 0000 0001 1982 6820. Sixteen bibliographic records are linked to her as author.
