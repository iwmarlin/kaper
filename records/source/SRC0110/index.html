Citation

Archiwum Uniwersytetu Warszawskiego, Bronisław Kaper student file, RP 6854. Aggregate archival source for documents from Kaper's University of Warsaw student file, including the young portrait, University of Warsaw matriculation and study records, and the police certificate of 21 April 1920 recording his religion as Mosaic. Consulted in the archive; no public URL.
