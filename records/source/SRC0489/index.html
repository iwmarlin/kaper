Verification note

The description follows the item-level entry in the earlier APAM-1 finding aid, not a direct examination or digitised reproduction of the score. The date 1936 is the copyright year of the described edition. The current UCLA catalogue identifies the collection as Walter Jurmann Papers, Collection 2271, and retains the French San Francisco entry in Box 1, Folder 9.
