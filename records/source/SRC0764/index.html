Citation

Henri Garat, printed as “Henry Garat”, with orchestra conducted by Kaper. “Le plus petit baiser”, from Une femme au volant. Waltz; words by L. Poterat, music by Kaper and Jurmann. Salabert 3382, matrix S.S.1728, n.d. Disc label photograph and audio transfer published by lysgauty1 collection disques David Silvestre on YouTube.

Discographic note

The readable label identifies Salabert 3382, S.S.1728, Henry Garat, the composers and lyricist, and “Orch. sous la dir. du compositeur Kaper”. It also prints Éditions Echo; that wording is not treated as a synonym of the Salabert record imprint. The uploader attaches 3 October 1933 to the disc without explicitly distinguishing a recording date from an issue date, so it is retained only as a reported date of the described item. SRC0300 records the BnF description of the coupled Salabert 3382 issue as [1934]; the exact photographed pressing is not dated from that entry. The upload was published on 18 October 2021. The earlier phrase identifying it as the same physical pressing held by BnF was not supported.
