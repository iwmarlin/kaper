Citation

Robert Péguy. [Statement on Édouard Herriot’s film decree], with a portrait captioned “M. Robert Péguy.” La Critique cinématographique: publication corporative hebdomadaire (Paris), no. 67, 3 March 1928, p. 8. Marpon & Cie. Bibliothèque nationale de France / Gallica. ARK: ark:/12148/bpt6k4548254t.

Open source ↗
Object context

Péguy writes as a working French director welcoming the protective decree, and gives his own position: the cinema is an art but above all a force, and France should regain the rank it lost through the fault of some and the improvidence of others. He mentions the recent presentation of his film Paris-New-York-Paris. No photographer is credited beside the portrait.
