Identity assessment

The date is lettered on the print as 7-18-24. The view runs north from the Gansevoort Street market sheds along the Chelsea Piers, the line of piers from 54 to 62 to which the original Pier 57, built in 1907 and destroyed by fire in 1947, belonged. The print does not number the piers, so the record does not mark which shed is Pier 57.
