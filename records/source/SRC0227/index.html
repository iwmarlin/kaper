Citation

Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin / StabiKat, “Cocktails” linked sheet-music records, parent/cluster record no. 1378200438, accessed via KVK. The linked records comprise 11 sheet-music entries issued in Berlin by Alberti or Alrobi, 1929–32, including Bronisław Kaper, “Fiesta: <Bianca>: Rumba: Transcr. f. 2 Pianos” (Alberti, [1932]); Bronisław Kaper, “Austrian Cocktail: Foxtrot Transcription on famous Strauss melodies: For 2 Pianos” (Alrobi, [1930]); and Bronisław Kaper, “Russian Cocktail: Foxtrot Transcription on celebrates Tschaikowsky melodies: For 2 Pianos” (Alrobi, [1930]). Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin, Musikabteilung, Unter den Linden.
