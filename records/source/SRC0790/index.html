Discographic note

Uploader-supplied information, not transcribed from the disc label: The upload calls the Odeon issue the first release of the recording and shows a portrait of the bandleader from a Funk-Almanach rather than the disc. The discographic details are taken from the uploader's description; the label is not shown in the external recording. The Odeon matrix book, indexed at musiktiteldb.de, places the recording in Berlin, Schlesische Straße 26, room 4, on 10 June 1932 under matrix Be 9946, coupled with “Heute Nacht oder nie!”. The label credits the music to Bob Henders alone, the recording name under which Walter Jurmann worked with Kaper.
