Discographic note

The label shown at the start of the video identifies Parlophon B 4502, prints the number 38185 and names Saxophon-Orchester Dobbri, singer Max Mensing and Dr. B. Kaper. The number 38185 is not treated here as an independently verified matrix or take. The uploader reports a Berlin recording in January 1930; the label does not establish its date or the manufacture date of the photographed copy. The structured date is the YouTube publication date. The label and upload describe the German song W-S008, not the Polish-language version.
