Citation

In 1000 Takte-Tanz, Band 2: 22 der populärsten Tanz- und Gesangs-Schlager, page 12; text, melody and piano setting. Berlin: Alrobi-Musikverlag GmbH / Edition Meisel & Co. GmbH, 1929.

Date assessment

The volume was previously dated here to about 1939. The song years of the collection and the trade record of the series place Band 2 in 1929, and the entry itself is dated 1929.
