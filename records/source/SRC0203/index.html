Citation

1000 Takte-Tanz, Band 2: 22 der populärsten Tanz- und Gesangs-Schlager. Berlin: Alrobi-Musikverlag, 1929. Piano-vocal score; “Wenn wir nicht beide in Gesellschaft wären, würde ich dir Liebe schwören (Vergißmeinnicht),” music by Bronisław Kaper and words by Fritz Rotter, appears on p. 12. Alfred Grunwald Papers, *T-Mss 1998-030, series IV, box 17, folder 1, Billy Rose Theatre Division, The New York Public Library for the Performing Arts.

Date assessment

The volume was previously dated here to about 1939. Contemporary trade evidence places Band 2 in 1929. The NYPL finding aid documents a physical piano-vocal copy in box 17, folder 1; SRC0787 remains the separate Deutsches Lied index record for the song and its page-level metadata.
