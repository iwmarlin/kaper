Citation

Alfred Beres mit Orchester, with refrain sung by Eric Helgar. “Nur Tango, nur Tango,” tango from the sound film Alraune; music by Dr. B. Kaper, words by Fr. Rotter. Ultraphon A 744, matrix 15875, coupled with “Wenn du mal in Hawaii bist …”. One 25 cm shellac disc at 78 rpm, issued by N.V. Küchenmeister’s Internationale Ultraphoon Maatschappij, Amsterdam and Berlin. Deutsche Nationalbibliothek, Deutsches Musikarchiv, record 381098265. Accessed 25 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Discographic note

The catalogue states no recording or issue date; the year is supplied from the film, whose songs were published in Berlin in 1930, and is not transcribed from the disc. The label credit is given by the catalogue as “Alfred Beres mit Orchester, Refraingesang: Eric Helgar”, with the generic description “Tanzorchester mit Refraingesang”. The same library holds a second issue of the song, Decca F 40007, matrix FGB 2355, played by The Rhythm Maniacs with a German refrain sung by Leo Mond and coupled with “In meinem Herzen ist nur Platz für eine”.
