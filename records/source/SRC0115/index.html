Citation

Henry Garat, vocal; orchestra conducted by Bronisław Kaper. “Pourvu qu’on ait vingt ans,” from Une femme au volant. Music by Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann; words by Louis Poterat. Salabert 3384, 78 rpm disc, 7 November 1933. YouTube transfer from the private 78 rpm collection of David Silvestre, channel lysgauty1, video JA57rCoKoyI. Discographic context checked against Gérard Roig, “Discographie d’Henry Garat,” Phonoscopies no. 5 (January 1994): 11, which lists Salabert 3384 for the coupled side “Ninon, quand tu me souris.”
