Citation

Société des auteurs, compositeurs et éditeurs de musique. “Jacques Monteux.” Musée SACEM, Les créateurs. Dialogue writer and lyricist, born in Paris on 5 November 1899, died 7 April 1979; admitted to SACEM on 2 March 1926 and elected full member on 8 April 1933. Accessed 25 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Authority note

The biography spells the surname “Monteaux” while the heading of the same record, the membership photograph and the authority files read Monteux. The dates of birth and death agree with the authority files to the day. Admission on 2 March 1926 places the start of his registered authorship seven years before the two Kaper and Jurmann songs he wrote for the French version of “Madame ne veut pas d’enfants”.
