Evidence recorded in this source

The entry lists the credits recorded with the registration: Harold S. Bucquet as director, and no composer. The same page lists Paul Terry’s Little Boy Blue of 1933 and a Universal film of 1916; Ub Iwerks’s ComiColor cartoon of 1936 has no entry in the catalogue.
