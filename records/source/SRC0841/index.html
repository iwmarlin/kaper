Identity assessment

The caption settles the identification: it presents him as “a fellow songwriter” beside Hoagy Carmichael, which fits the lyricist of the Broadway and Hollywood years and not a namesake, and it places him in the Californian desert resort where he lived out his later life. The occasion is a bar opening, not a professional gathering, and the picture is the only likeness of him gathered here; it stands twenty-seven years after the song of his that enters the Kaper record.
