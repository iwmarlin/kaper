Discographic note

The discographic details were catalogued from the disc by The Great 78 Project; the transfer carries sound alone, so the label is not shown in the external recording. The item records the matrix number OA09937 and places the song in the film „Everybody Sing“; the other side of the disc carries “The One I Love”. The American issue of the same session is Victor 4381, recorded on 13 January 1938.
