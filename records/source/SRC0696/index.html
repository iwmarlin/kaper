Citation

Jazz-Orchester. “Das verliebte Orchester.” Foxtrot; label credit “Musik und Text v. Dr. B. Kaper & Fr. Rotter”. Rot-Blau, Bestell-Nr. 9613, n.d. The uploader identifies the band as Paul von Beky und sein Orchester, billed on the label only as Jazz-Orchester, and gives 1931 as the year of issue; the label prints neither. The label also carries the mark C. 15 and the B.I.E.M. stamp. Shellac disc from the Schallarchiv of the Radiomuseum Hardthausen, its label filmed in the YouTube upload.
