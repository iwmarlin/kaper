Może tak, może nie / Pokoik na Hożej — Columbia DM 1624

Sound-recording catalogue

Citation

“Może tak, może nie = Gib nur Acht, über Nacht kommt die Liebe : boston / F. Rotter, Kaper ; Oldlen. Pokoik na Hożej : piosenka / W. Dan ; Oldlen.” Zofia Terné, vocal, with the Columbia orchestra. [Warsaw]: Columbia, [ca 1935]. One 78 rpm disc, 25 cm, publisher’s number DM 1624. Biblioteka Narodowa, Magazyn Zbiorów Dźwiękowych i Audiowizualnych, shelfmarks Fon.K.290 and Fon.II.17.599 A; MMS ID 991043120839705066, OCLC 947182714. Language given as Polish. The catalogue enters Julian Tuwim as author of the words (“Sł.”), printed on the label as Oldlen, and Kaper and Rotter as composers of the song and of its German original.

Date assessment

The Biblioteka Narodowa publication statement gives [ca 1935], where Stare Melodie gives 1932 for the same disc. Neither states whether its year dates the recording or the issue, so the two are kept apart and neither is substituted for the other.
