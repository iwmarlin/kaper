Citation

“Valéry e Claudel. Uma conferência no ‘Lycée Français’.” Diário de Notícias (Rio de Janeiro), ano I, no. 36, 17 July 1930, p. 15. The item announces a lecture by Ninon Steinhoff on Paul Valéry and Paul Claudel and reproduces her portrait with the caption “Sra. Ninon Steinhof.” No photographer is credited.
