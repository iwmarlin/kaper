Citation

Richard Tauber, tenor, accompanied under Paul Dessau. “Schade, dass Liebe ein Märchen ist”, from the film Melodie der Liebe. On Richard Tauber Vol. 1, Membran Music Ltd., released 15 April 2010, distributed by Kontor New Media; the release credits the song to Jurmann, Kaper and Rotter and names Musikverlag Alrobi as its publisher. A modern transfer of Tauber's recording, made as he was filming Melodie der Liebe; the release gives neither catalogue nor matrix number for the original disc. Credits as supplied by the distributor to the channel. Accessed 21 August 2026.
