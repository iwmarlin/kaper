Verification note

The entry lists “À Paris tiguidiguidi” as a 1933 song written by Louis Poterat and Walter Jurmann, performed by Henry Garat. The page cites Jean-Claude Marcadet, 700 chansons de Paris (Paris: Parigramme, 2012), but the cited book entry has not been directly consulted.
