Discographic note

DNB record 380669358 describes the coupled “Oh Madonna!” side, with Be.10220-2 and WBe.10220-2 as matrix forms; its field 777 explicitly links “Ninon” as record 380669366. The primary URL follows that link, not a guessed identifier. The separate GHT matrix discography identifies “Ninon” as Be 10221, recorded in Berlin on 7 February 1933 with Jan Kiepura and Frieder Weissmann. The structured date describes that session, not a DNB issue date. Neither the coupled side’s take suffix nor the matrix is transferred to “Ninon”. The corrected catalogue identifier is established by DNB’s coupling metadata; direct retrieval of the linked record was rate-limited during this review, and the full GHT PDF was unavailable for download.
