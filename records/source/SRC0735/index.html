Adieu, es ist zu schön, um wahr zu sein — Odeon A 169240 a

Recording or discographic source

Citation

Bernhard Etté mit seinem Tanz-Orchester, vocal by Wolfgang Schmidt. “Adieu, es ist zu schön, um wahr zu sein.” Tango from Abenteuer am Lido; label credits music to Jurmann and Kaper and words to Fritz Rotter. Odeon A 169240 a, printed matrix Bi 1641. Disc label shown in the YouTube transfer published by Radiomuseum Hardthausen.

Discographic note

The displayed label is Odeon A 169240 a, with matrix Bi 1641, Bernhard Etté’s dance orchestra and Wolfgang Schmidt. The upload describes a 1933 Lindström recording issued on Gloria in Germany and Odeon abroad; the displayed copy itself bears an Odeon label. The year remains the uploader-reported recording year, not an independently established issue date. The label does not establish a take or the relationship between particular Gloria and Odeon pressings.
