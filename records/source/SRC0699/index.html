Citation

Hans Albers with the Odeon-Künstler-Orchester under Otto Dobrindt. “Immer wenn ich glücklich bin.” Foxtrot from the Boston sound film Heut’ kommt’s drauf an; label credits music to Walter Jurmann and B. Kaper and words to Fritz Rotter. Odeon O-11811 a, matrix Be 10226, 78 rpm, 1933. Filmed-label transfer published on YouTube by Schellackplatte on 21 May 2008. Accessed 15 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Object context

The recording date of 13 February 1933, Berlin studio 2, and the April 1933 release follow the Odeon label list at musiktiteldb.de. The filmed copy carries a C. A. Klemm dealer sticker and a notice prohibiting radio transmission without permission.
