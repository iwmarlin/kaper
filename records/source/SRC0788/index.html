Discographic note

The discographic details were catalogued from the disc by The Great 78 Project; the transfer carries sound alone, so the label is not shown in the external recording. The item records the matrix number Be 9887 and states that the side is sung in German, from the film released in English as The Right to Happiness. That matrix belongs to Tauber’s Berlin session of 5 April 1932, issued in Germany as Odeon O-4507 under the German title; the English title belongs to the export pressing, not to an English text. The reverse side, matrix Be 9889, carries “Es war einmal ein Baby” under the English title “Once There Was a Baby”.
