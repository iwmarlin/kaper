Discographic note

The Internet Archive item includes a photograph of the Australian Parlophone AR 206 label. It names Richard Tauber and conductor Paul Dessau, prints matrix Be 9887 and explicitly states “Sung in German”, despite the English song and film titles. The runout identifies take 2. The item links the reverse side as “Once There Was a Baby”. No issue date is printed or supplied in the item metadata. The earlier year 1932 and the date of the German recording session do not establish when this Australian pressing was issued; its issue date remains unknown. Patent dates around the rim are not issue dates.
