Oh Madonna! — Odeon

Recording Discographic Source

Citation

Jan Kiepura with Odeon Künstler-Orchester. “Oh Madonna!” Music by Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann; words by Fritz Rotter and Ernst Marischka; from the sound film Ein Lied für dich. Odeon, shellac 78 rpm, 1933. Internet Archive, George Blood Collection, digitized 78 rpm recording.
