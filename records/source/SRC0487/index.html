Oh Madonna! — Odeon O-4707 a

Recording or discographic source

Citation

Jan Kiepura with the Odeon-Künstler-Orchester conducted by Dr. Weissmann. “Oh Madonna!”, from Ein Lied für dich; label credits music to B. Kaper and Walter Jurmann and words to Fritz Rotter and Ernst Marischka. Odeon O-4707 a, matrix Be 10220, take 2; recorded 11 February 1933. Disc label photograph and audio transfer published by the Great 78 Project, Internet Archive.

Discographic note

The photograph identifies Odeon O-4707 a, matrix Be 10220, and the runout shows take 2. Christian Zwarg’s Odeon matrix discography lists Be 10220-2 under 11 February 1933, Berlin, Schlesische Straße 26, room 2, with Jan Kiepura and Frieder Weissmann. The indexed PDF text was consulted; the complete file could not be retrieved during this review. Internet Archive instead supplies 1932 without a stated basis; that year conflicts with the matrix chronology and is not adopted. The structured date describes the recording session, not the issue or manufacture of this copy. Henry König’s Odeon label list separately lists O-4707 under 1933 but leaves the release-date column empty. The label does not identify Wiener Boheme-Verlag as the sheet-music publisher.
