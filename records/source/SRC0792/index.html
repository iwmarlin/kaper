Discographic note

The entry identifies the song as a 1933 Austrian foxtrot from the film released in Germany as “Madame wünscht keine Kinder”, credits the music to Walter Jurmann and Dr. B. Kaper and the words to Fritz Rotter and Max Kolpe, and lists two commercial issues: Artiphon 8489 by Eddy Walis and Gloria G.O. 10725 by Fred Bird.
