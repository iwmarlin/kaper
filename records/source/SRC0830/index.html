Object context

The photograph carries no photographer’s or agency’s credit, unlike the studio portraits the paper usually printed. It shows the director at work rather than posed: he is crouching beside the camera, giving a signal, with the operator behind him. The interview belongs to a series in which directors were asked what the coming of sound had changed; Choux argues that speech has given man and his words a disproportionate share of the screen.
