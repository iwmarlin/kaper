Online Database

Citation

Société des auteurs, compositeurs et éditeurs de musique. “René Nazelles.” Musée SACEM, Les créateurs. Author and composer, born in Paris on 8 October 1888, died on 11 September 1975; elected full member of SACEM on 13 September 1911; Prix Salabert, 1958.
