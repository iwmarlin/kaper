Verification note

The manifest is the primary record behind the arrival: the non-immigrant visas issued in Paris on 15 October 1934 in one consecutive series with the Jurmanns’ (NI 1581–1584), Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer as payer of the passage, “musician” in the calling or occupation field, and Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer at Culver City as the party being joined. The citation, including list 7 and line 1, is transcribed from footnote 40 of the study that read the manifest. The arrival record resolves at the identifier given; the manifest image has not been examined here, so the list and line rest on that study rather than on a second reading.
