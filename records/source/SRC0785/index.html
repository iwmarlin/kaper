Eric Harden’s danseorkester, refrain sung by Carljohann Volbach. “Wie gern möcht’ ich dich verwöhnen.” From the sound film Melodie der Liebe; catalogue number D 4431, matrix Bi 644, 1932. Digital transfer published on archive.org from the 78 rpm collection of Leif Druedahl. Accessed 24 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Discographic note

The item gives the catalogue number D 4431 and the matrix number Bi 644 but does not name the label, and the transfer carries sound alone. The matrix identifies the master as the Gloria issue G.O. 10303, recorded in Berlin, studio 4, on 11 June 1932 and released in November 1932 according to the title list at musiktiteldb.de; the Danish wording of the performer credit belongs to the pressing, not to that recording session.
