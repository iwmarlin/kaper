Citation

“Retour des Baléares. A bord du navire: Bourdelle, le metteur en scène Serge de Poligny et Jean Gabin.” Photograph. Pour vous: l’hebdomadaire du cinéma (Paris), 9 March 1933, p. 7. Bibliothèque nationale de France / RetroNews.

Open source ↗
Object context

The caption names the three men on deck and calls de Poligny the director, without naming a film. The occasion can be identified: in 1933 he directed L’Étoile de Valencia, a French version made for the European branch of Universum-Film, whose story runs from a cabaret in Palma to a ship of the same name, with Thomy Bourdelle as Captain Mendoza and Jean Gabin as the ship’s mechanic. The photograph therefore shows the unit returning from the Balearic locations of that film, four months after the French release of the picture he had made with music by Kaper and Jurmann. No photographer or agency is credited beside it.
