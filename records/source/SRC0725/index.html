Citation

Austin Egen, accompanied by the Odeon-Tango-Orchester. “Ich weiß ein kleines Café.” Tango; label credits “Tango (Dr. B. Kaper und Austin Egen)” and “Text: Fritz Rotter”, with the note “Auch als Tanzplatte geeignet”. Odeon O-11439 a, matrix Be 9409, 78 R, B.I.E.M., with “Carl Lindström A.-G.” printed along the foot of the label, n.d. Label filmed in the YouTube upload by Ilja Livschakoff.
