Evidence recorded in this source

The ASCAP tabulation ranks “You’re All I Need” fourteenth with 21,424 network broadcasts. It prints the source title as Lie Like Gentlemen (Escapade), Walter Jurmann’s surname as “Jarmann,” and credits Bronisław Kaper, Walter Jurmann and Gus Kahn; the publisher is Robbins.
