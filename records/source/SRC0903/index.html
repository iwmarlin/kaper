Evidence recorded in this source

The listing reads: “Bach-Saal, Sonnab., 3. Dezember, abends 8 Uhr Klavier-Abend F. Chopin (4 Balladen), Szymanowski, B. Kaper, Ravel, Albeniz, Liszt (H-moll).” It fixes the hall, the date and the programme, and names Kaper among the composers without naming his work. It does not describe any item as a first performance: the words zum ersten Male and Uraufführung do not appear, although the guide uses Neuaufführungen elsewhere in the same issue.
