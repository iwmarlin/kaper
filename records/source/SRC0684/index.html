Citation

Uwe Harten. "Gmeindl, Walter." Oesterreichisches Musiklexikon online. Vienna: Verlag der Österreichischen Akademie der Wissenschaften. doi:10.1553/0x0001cf41. The article gives 31 December 1890 at Hainburg an der Donau as the date of birth and 1 September 1958 at Hainburg as the date of death, and traces the appointments at the Munich Hofoper from 1915, the teaching post under Schreker in Berlin from 1922 and the professorship of composition from 1929. The lexicon spells the forename Walter, without the h carried by this record. Accessed 6 August 2026.

Evidence recorded in this source

The article calls Gmeindl a pedagogue before anything else. Schreker engaged him in Berlin as a Dozent in 1922 and, in 1929, as professor of composition and head of the orchestra school of the Musikhochschule, where he later took the conducting class as well; Sergiu Celibidache and Ernst Pepping were among his pupils. It is the teaching post held during Kaper's Berlin years, which the archive documents in the event of private study, 1926–1930.
