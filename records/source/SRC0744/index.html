Citation

Mitja Nikisch und sein Orchester, refrain sung by Paul Dorn. “Ich steh’ im Schnee und wart’ auf Dich.” Foxtrot from Die grosse Attraktion. Electrola E.G. 2228, matrix OD106-2, recorded Berlin, 15 March 1931; also issued as His Master's Voice AM 3411 and R 14602. Electrola label and title lists at musiktiteldb.de, following the Discographie der deutschen Tanzmusik, vol. 4. Accessed 21 August 2026.

Open source ↗
Evidence recorded in this source

The discographic entry assigns both words and music to Bronisław Kaper. The published sheet music instead credits music to Kaper and Walter Jurmann and words to Fritz Rotter and Armin Robinson; the discographic entry is not used to replace those work-level credits.
