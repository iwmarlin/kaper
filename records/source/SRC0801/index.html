Citation

Tino Rossi, voice, with Orchestre de Tango Rafaelo. “Les nuits moscovites,” sung waltz, and “On n’aime qu’une fois,” sung tango, from Les nuits moscovites; music by Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann. Columbia DF 1622, matrices CL 5068 and CL 5093. One 25 cm 78 rpm disc. Bibliothèque nationale de France, Catalogue général, notice FRBNF37826068, ARK ark:/12148/cb378260686.

Discographic note

The BnF catalogue dates the disc only as “19..”. The supplied date [1934] follows the film’s release year and is stated as supplied rather than transcribed. The catalogue lists matrices CL 5068 and CL 5093 but does not assign each matrix to a title in the displayed metadata, so no side-level matrix assignment is inferred here.
