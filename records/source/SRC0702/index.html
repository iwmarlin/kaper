Citation

Willi Domgraf-Fassbaender with the Marek Weber Orchestra conducted by Marek Weber. “Der gluckliche Herr Funf: Schon ist das ganze Leben.” Music by Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann; words by Fritz Rotter. On Schlager und Filmmelodien von Walter Jurmann, vol. 2 (Recordings 1929–1936). Jube Pops, released 5 February 2013; YouTube art track supplied by Naxos Digital Services and published 11 February 2015.
