Date assessment

The Boston Public Library dates the card to about 1930–1945, the span of Tichnor’s linen-finish cards. The back prints the hotel’s own advertisement, “Culver Hotel, ‘A Family Hotel!’, Washington at Culver Blvd.”, with the Tichnor Art Company imprint and no copyright notice.
