Channel Incident: the production of a Ministry of Information film, UK, September 1940. Anthony Asquith directs Peggy Ashcroft and Gordon Harker in a film about the evacuation of Dunkirk, made by Denham and Pinewood Studios for the Ministry of Information. Photograph D 1076A, Ministry of Information Second World War Official Collection, Imperial War Museums. Accessed 1 August 2026.
