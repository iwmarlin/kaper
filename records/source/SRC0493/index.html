Citation

Jazzorchester John Morris, with vocal refrain by Erik Garell. “Ich weiß, daß du einst meinen Mund vergißt” (2216), music by K. M. May and Dr. B. Kaper, words by Fr. Rotter and K. Brüll / “Du lieber Geiger, spiel einen Tango für mich” (2222), music by Dr. B. Kaper and German, words by Fritz Rotter. Both from Seitensprünge. Adler Electro, Bestell-Nr. 5713, 10-inch 78 rpm shellac disc, Germany, n.d. Discogs release 2950735; photographs of both labels.

Discographic note

Both photographed labels identify the performers and authors, Bestell-Nr. 5713 and the additional side numbers 2216 and 2222. The Discogs record supplies no issue date; the film’s 1931 date does not establish when this disc was released. The labels do not identify Edition Karl Brüll as publisher. References to Gloria and an unrelated YouTube transfer have been removed: this source documents the photographed Adler Electro issue.
