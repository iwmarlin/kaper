Evidence recorded in this source

A secondary work list, arranged by year. By its own note it follows the Walter Jurmann website, so it does not corroborate the online works catalogue (SRC0306) independently. It is a different work from Alexander Sieghardt’s catalogue in Buxbaum (SRC0373); until November 2026 the archive’s notes took it for Sieghardt’s “year-by-year list”.
