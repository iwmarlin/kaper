Jan Kiepura podczas pobytu w Paryżu, November 1933

Archival Photograph

Citation

Photographs from the series ‘Jan Kiepura podczas pobytu w Paryżu’ (Jan Kiepura during his stay in Paris), November 1933, including a reception photograph with Bronisław Kaper and his wife Eleonora. Narodowe Archiwum Cyfrowe (NAC), Koncern Ilustrowany Kurier Codzienny – Archiwum Ilustracji, ref. 3/1/0/11/8270 (image 3/1/0/11/8270/3). Also catalogued on Szukaj w Archiwach as unit 5924863, object 374619.
