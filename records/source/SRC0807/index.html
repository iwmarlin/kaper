Fred Marley und sein Tanz-Orchester, with Leo Monosson identified as refrain vocalist in the discography. “Eine Sommernacht am Meer.” Grammophon 885 B, matrix 1703 BN3; recorded at the Schumannsaal, Berlin, 15 August 1932. Music credited as Bob Handers [the joint pseudonym of Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann]; words by Fritz Rotter. Labelliste „Grammophon“ (1931–1932), based on Discographie der deutschen Tanzmusik, vol. 8. Accessed 24 August 2026.
