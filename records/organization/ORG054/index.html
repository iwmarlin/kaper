Alberti GmbH

Heading source
local heading
City
Berlin
Country

Note

Music and theatre publishing house of Viktor Alberti (1884–1942), a Hungarian-born publisher and music dealer working in Berlin; the house issued the first German jazz periodical, Musik-Echo, between 1930 and 1934. No name register carries the firm — the GND holds only Max Alberti's Verlag of Hanau, a different house — so the heading is a local one.
