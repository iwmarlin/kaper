Note

Music publisher owned by Franz Sobotka. Historical imprints use the forms Sirius Verlag Franz Sobotka and Sirius-Verlag F. Sobotka; the house issued the Sirius-Mappen and the successor periodical Tonfilm Theater Tanz. No matching formal GND or VIAF corporate authority has been identified.
