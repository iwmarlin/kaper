Note

Music publishing house in Vienna and Berlin. It issued the Sirius-Mappen in six annual series from 1927 to January 1933, and from 1933 the successor publication Tonfilm Theater Tanz, which ran to 1943. No name register carries the firm: the GND holds three bodies called Sirius, one German, one unplaced and one an Austrian company registered under a legal form introduced in 2007, and none of them is this house.
