Note

German recording company active from 1925 to 1972; the linked discs use Electrola as their imprint. In 1972 Electrola-Gesellschaft and Carl Lindström GmbH were merged into the successor company EMI Electrola. The designation E.G. belongs to a record series, not to the organization’s name.
