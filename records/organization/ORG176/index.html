Encyclopédie multimédia de la comédie musicale théâtrale en France

Database
Country
France
Name variants
ECMF; ecmf.fr

Note

Encyclopedia of the musical comedies staged in Parisian theatres between 1918 and 1944, with pages for works, authors, composers, performers, publishers and theatres.
