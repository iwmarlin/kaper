About this place

Culver City is the location of the MGM studios where Kaper worked during his Hollywood career.

Documented events4

  • Hollywood debut 1935
  • Everybody Sing with Gus Kahn 13 July 1937–17 March 1938
  • Kaper’s solo career at MGM 1939
  • MGM markets Kaper and Jurmann as “Viennese composers” 20 November–8 December 1934
