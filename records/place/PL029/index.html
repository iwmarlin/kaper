Summary

French Norman Revival apartment building completed in 1927 and designated Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument no. 799. Kaper’s 1 November 1935 immigration manifest records 6626 Franklin Avenue as his U.S. destination, and his Declaration of Intention dated 4 February 1936 records the same address…
