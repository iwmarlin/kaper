About this place

New York hotel where Kaper and his wife Eleonora stayed on arrival from Le Havre before travelling on to Los Angeles. In his 1975 AFI oral history Kaper recalled the Edison Hotel as the studio's $300-a-week accommodation tier.

Documented events1

  • Arrival in New York en route to Los Angeles 24 October 1934
