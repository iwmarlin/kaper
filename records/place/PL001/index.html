About this place

Kaper’s birthplace and the main city of his education and early musical activity before his move to Berlin.

  • Birth 23 January / 5 February 1902
  • Graduation from a primary school 1915
  • High school graduation 1919
  • Formal matriculation at the University of Warsaw 6 February 1920
  • Baptism 17 July 1920
  • War 1920
  • Music studies at the Chopin Music School in Warsaw c. 1920–1925
  • First composition 1923
  • Application to Faculty of Philosophy 1924
  • Official completion of law studies 14 February–26 May 1925
  • Public performance at the Chopin Music School concert 1925
  • First cabaret songs published 1926
  • Move to Berlin c. 1926
  • Performance of piano pieces 1928
  • University admission delayed by a disciplinary sanction 18 October 1919 / 6 February 1920
  • Police certificate records Mosaic religion 21 April 1920
