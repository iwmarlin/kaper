About this place

Concert hall in the pre-war Warsaw Conservatory building at 1 Okólnik Street, associated with the 1928 press notices on Eliza Rozenblum’s performance of Kaper’s piano pieces. The coordinates indicate the conservatory site, not the exact hall point.

Documented events1

  • Performance of piano pieces 1928
