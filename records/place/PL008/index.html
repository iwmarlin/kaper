About this place

University of Warsaw campus associated with Kaper's law studies. The marker identifies the historic central university site at campus level; it does not claim a particular Faculty of Law room or classroom.

Documented events4

  • Formal matriculation at the University of Warsaw 6 February 1920
  • Application to Faculty of Philosophy 1924
  • Official completion of law studies 14 February–26 May 1925
  • University admission delayed by a disciplinary sanction 18 October 1919 / 6 February 1920
