About this place

Vienna functions here as a European music-film and publicity context for the Berlin–Paris–Hollywood route and the star-singer circuit. MGM’s later “Viennese composers” label is not presented as Kaper’s biographical origin.

Documented events4

  • Migration from Berlin via Vienna to Paris 1933
  • The Sängerfilm and the star-singer circuit 1931–1933
  • Multiple-language versions and transnational sound-film production 1931–1933
  • MGM markets Kaper and Jurmann as “Viennese composers” 20 November–8 December 1934
