About this place

Land border port of entry linking Calexico (California) and Mexicali (Baja California), documented as the point of Kaper and Eleonora's 1935 permanent-residence entry into the United States. The coordinates mark the historic downtown crossing at the foot of Heffernan Avenue, where the 1933 inspection station stood in 1935; the vehicle crossing was moved two blocks west in 1974 and the present buildings are later.

UCLA suggests a possible connection to two Los Angeles Times reports from Calexico, “No Permit Needed at Mexican Border” of 6 July 1927 and “New Regulations in Force at Border” of 14 July 1927, but does not confirm publication.
