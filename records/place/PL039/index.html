About this place

Kaper’s 1 November 1935 Calexico manifest records the Commercial Hotel in Mexicali as his last permanent residence abroad. Historical evidence identifies the Hotel Comercial as the renamed Hotel Aldrete on Avenida Francisco I. Madero, between Ayuntamiento and José Azueta, beside Parque Héroes de Chapultepec. The marker represents that documented site; 301 is the present-day parcel number, not a verified 1935 street number.
