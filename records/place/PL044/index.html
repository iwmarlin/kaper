About this place

Southern French city where a jazz orchestra performed the Jurmann–Kaper song “Ninon, quand tu me souris” (from Tout pour l’amour) on an open-air stage at a summer festival on 22 June 1934, documenting the song’s move into the French concert/festival repertoire beyond cinema.
