About this place

Fryderyk Chopin Higher School of Music at the Warsaw Music Society, associated with Kaper’s piano studies and housed in the Warsaw Philharmonic building at Jasna 5. The marker identifies the documented building site, not a specific classroom.

Documented events2

  • Music studies at the Chopin Music School in Warsaw c. 1920–1925
  • Public performance at the Chopin Music School concert 1925
