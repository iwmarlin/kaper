About this place

Historic MGM studio complex in Culver City associated with Kaper's MGM career and Hollywood film work. The marker represents the studio site as a whole; the surviving evidence does not identify a particular stage, office or entrance for the linked events.

Documented events4

  • Hollywood debut 1935
  • Everybody Sing with Gus Kahn 13 July 1937–17 March 1938
  • New chapter 1939
  • MGM markets Kaper and Jurmann as “Viennese composers” 20 November–8 December 1934
