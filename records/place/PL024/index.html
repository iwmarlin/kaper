About this place

Kaper and Eleonora arrived in New York after crossing the Atlantic from Le Havre aboard the S.S. Champlain, before travelling on to Los Angeles.

Documented events2

  • Arrival in New York en route to Los Angeles 24 October 1934
  • Duke Ellington records “All God’s Chillun Got Rhythm” 8 June 1937
