About this place

Approximate arrival site for Kaper and Eleonora's 1934 Atlantic crossing from Le Havre aboard the S.S. Champlain. In this period the French Line (Compagnie Générale Transatlantique) used Pier 57 on the North River, at the foot of West 15th Street; the pier is inferred from the line's documented berth rather than named in the passenger manifest. The record establishes arrival at New York but not processing at Ellis Island.

Documented events1

  • Arrival in New York en route to Los Angeles 24 October 1934
