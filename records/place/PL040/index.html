About this place

Polish consular institution in Paris that issued Kaper’s Polish passport in August 1934, later noted in the Calexico border-crossing documentation. Historical address identified as 19 rue Alphonse-de-Neuville, Paris 17e.

Documented events2

  • Arrival in New York en route to Los Angeles 24 October 1934
  • Legal Permanent Entry 1 November 1935
