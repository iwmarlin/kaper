Summary

French port city context for Kaper's 1934 Atlantic crossing from Le Havre to New York aboard the S.S. Champlain. The marker is placed at city level: in 1934 CGT liners still used the port's earlier transatlantic quays, since the landmark Gare Maritime on the quai Johannès-Couvert opened only in May…
