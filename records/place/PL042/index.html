Kabarett der Komiker, Berlin

City
Berlin
Country
Germany
Type
venue
Period
European · 1926–1934

Summary

Berlin cabaret venue connected with Kaper’s paid work as a pianist. A July 1928 programme lists Sula Lewitsch and Bronisław Kaper as “Jazz auf zwei Klavieren”; because the event predates the later WOGA venue, Haus Cumberland / Palmenhaus at Kurfürstendamm 193–194 is retained as the probable site.

Sources
