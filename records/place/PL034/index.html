About this place

Documented Berlin residence: the Berliner Adreßbuch 1932 lists B. Kaper, composer, at Pommersche Straße 3 in Wilmersdorf. A later official planning record identifies the historical corner parcel as Sächsische Straße 48–49 / Pommersche Straße 3–4. Numbers 3–4 are absent from the present official address register, so the marker locates the historical corner parcel approximately rather than a surviving entrance.
