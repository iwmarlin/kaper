About this place

Berlin concert venue where Róża Etkin gave a recital on 3 December 1927 that included the first performance of a work by B. Kaper. The venue is identifiable with the former Blüthner-Saal, renamed Bach-Saal after renovation in autumn 1927, in the Klindworth-Scharwenka / Schwalm hall complex at Lützowstraße 76 / Genthiner Straße. The marker represents the documented complex rather than a reconstructed footprint of the individual hall.
