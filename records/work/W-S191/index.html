About this work

Recorded in Pfau's lexicon of German dance music as a slow fox of 1931 by Kaper with words by Fritz Rotter. The lexicon lists one disc, numbered F 681 and played by the Dittke orchestra with a vocal refrain, and notes that further details are unknown; no transfer of it has been traced.
