About this work

The lyric is signed “P. Kuckuck”. Behind the name stood Fritz Rotter, who is credited with the film’s lyrics and who wrote its two other songs under his own name, on prints where Kaper and Jurmann appeared as “Ernst Martin”; the publisher’s house history states that Rotter signed this one number under the pseudonym, in the year all three had to leave Germany. GEMA and the Library of Congress still register Peter Kuckuck as a name of its own.
