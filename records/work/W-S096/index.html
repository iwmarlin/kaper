  1. SRC0300 1934

    Henri Garat, vocal; orchestra conducted by Bronisław Kaper. “À Paris, tiguidiguidi” / “Le plus petit baiser,” from the film Une femme au volant. [Paris]: [Nouveau Disque Salabert], [1934]. 1 disc: 78 rpm, 25 cm. Commercial reference Salabert 3382; matrices S.S. 1727 and S.S. 1728. Bibliothèque nationale de France, Catalogue général, notice no. FRBNF38020911, ARK ark:/12148/cb380209111. Date note: the BnF catalogue dates the disc 1932, while Une femme au volant is dated 1933. Because a disc of songs from the film cannot predate the film, the BnF year is doubtful; [1934] is an inferred issue date rather than a printed date. The discrepancy remains unresolved.
