Le bistrot du port

About this work

Valse written for the singer Lys Gauty, to words by Gaston Groener and A. Saudemont; the printed edition (Paris: Coda, cop. 1934) credits the music to Pierre Candel, a pseudonym standing here for Kaper and Jurmann together. In his 1975 AFI oral history Kaper recalled it as the last song he wrote in Paris, and said he never heard it there before leaving for the United States.
