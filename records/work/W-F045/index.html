About this work

IMDb lists “Marche militaire” from Les nuits moscovites (1934), with music by Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann, as heard uncredited in the film; no other source says so, and the credits of the time name Muir Mathieson as music director (the copyright registrations) and Arthur Benjamin for the music (Hollywood Spectator). The Walter Jurmann works catalogue and Görner’s work list, which follows it, record background music Jurmann and Kaper wrote for the film as not used; Sieghardt’s filmography names Arthur Benjamin and, with a question mark, Jurmann for the music.

Kaper and Jurmann are not credited as score composers. Their connection is quoted uncredited music: IMDb/SRC0173 identifies “Marche militaire” from Les nuits moscovites, music by Bronislau Kaper and Walter Jurmann. AFI/SRC0481 credits Arthur Benjamin as score composer and Muir Mathieson as music director. The Jurmann works catalogue (SRC0306) lists “Hintergrundmusik für Wings of the Morning … nicht verwendet”, in collaboration with Kaper.
