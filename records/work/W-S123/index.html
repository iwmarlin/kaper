  • Walter JurmannComposer
  • Georges Rey · printed as “G. Rey”
    Credited as composer alongside Kaper and Jurmann on the Tahitian-language version issued by Pathé-Marconi in 1940 as “O chant d'amour de Tahiti !” (Pathé PA 1234), not on the 1935 song. The Bibliothèque nationale de France distinguishes the roles deliberately on this disc: the coupling, “Ho'i-maï”, is credited to Rey as “comp. et par.”, while the Tahiti side gives all three men “comp.” and names no lyricist. Rey also performs on the record, leading Tihoti-ré et son ensemble tahitien and singing the vocal duo with Mlle Maéva under his Tahitian pseudonym Tihoti.    Composer
