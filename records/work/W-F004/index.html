About this work

Bronisław Kaper’s involvement is considered probable, but his role is not established. The only positive attribution is IMDb, which lists him as “Benjamin Kapper”; Filmportal and Walter Jurmann estate materials do not confirm it. Hofmeisters Musikalisch-literarischer Monatsbericht (July 1931): 152 registers the film’s song “Du bist nicht die Erste” under Walter Jurmann alone, and the film was shot in autumn 1930, before the Jurmann–Kaper collaboration began.
