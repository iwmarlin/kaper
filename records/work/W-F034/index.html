About this work

Song ‘Love Song of Tahiti’ (m. Kaper & Jurmann, lyric Gus Kahn) — uncredited on screen; published sheet music (SRC0486) carries the printed credit. The Walter Jurmann works catalogue (SRC0306) also records background music for the film by Kaper & Jurmann. Score: Herbert Stothart (SRC0168; SRC0373).
