About this work

Kaper and Jurmann contributed songs, not the film score. Press source SRC0114 gives music by Bronislaw Kaper and Walter Jurmann with lyrics by Gus Kahn; further song records and relations document their songs for the film. AFI (SRC0141) credits Franz Waxman as music director, not as composer of the score.
