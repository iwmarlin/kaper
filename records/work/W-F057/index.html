About this work

English-language version of Ein Lied für Dich, made in Britain by Gaumont British with Jan Kiepura in the leading role. Bronisław Kaper is not credited on the film; his documented connection is the reuse of “Ninon”, the song he wrote with Walter Jurmann for the German original.

Gives a 1934 British musical film directed by Maurice Elvey for Gaumont British and Ciné-Alliance, produced by Michael Balcon and Arnold Pressburger, starring Jan Kiepura, Sonnie Hale and Emlyn Williams, adapted from Ernst Marischka and Irma von Cube’s Ein Lied für Dich, with a score by Mischa Spoliansky and the title song by Spoliansky and Frank Eyton.
