About this work

Evidence points to more than one French publication context: Buxbaum/Sieghardt records Robbins/Francis Day, while a 1936 French-edition listing names Éditions France Mélodie. The catalogue prints “Didier Alcan Bloch,” whereas the French-edition listing credits Didier Mauprey. BnF confirms Didier Mauprey as a pseudonym of Didier Bloch but does not attest “Didier Alcan Bloch”; that identity resolution remains probable.
