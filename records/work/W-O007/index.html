About this work

The original “Cocktails” cover lists ten transcriptions in two booklets, five in each, and credits “Transcriptions by Dr. B. Kaper.” “Fiesta (Bianca),” published in 1932 under the heading “Ein neuer Cocktail,” is a later associated issue and therefore appears under Related works rather than in the original contents.

Contents of the original two-booklet edition

  1. Miss Annabelle Lee (jazz)Music by Sidney Cläre, Lew Pollack, Harry Richman
  2. Raquel (waltz)Music by Jesse Greer
  3. One Step to Heaven (foxtrot)Music by Jesse Greer
  4. Sunshine (slow-fox)Music by Irving Berlin
  5. Austrian Coctail (foxtrot transcription on famous Strauss melodies)
  6. Get Out and Get under the Moon (foxtrot)Music by L. Shay
  7. Sonny BoyMusic by Ray Henderson, Buddy DeSylva, and Lew Brown
  8. Russian Coctail (foxtrot transcription on celebrated Tschaikowsky melodies)
  9. The Wedding of the Painted Doll (foxtrot)
  10. Pagan Love Song: Singin' in the rain
