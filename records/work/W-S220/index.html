About this work

English-language version registered in London in 1932, with Fritz Rotter’s German words and music by Kaper and “German” (Fritz German, i.e. Hermann Scheibenhofer), the credits of “Du lieber Geiger, spiel einen Tango für mich”.

    “Oh, Mister Leader, Please Play a Tango for Me” is probably the English version of “Du lieber Geiger, spiel einen Tango für mich”: the registration names a tango with Fritz Rotter’s German words and music by Kaper and “German”, the credits of the German song, but does not name the original.
