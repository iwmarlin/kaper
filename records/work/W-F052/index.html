About this work

MGM one-reel Miniature after Eugene Field’s poem, directed by Harold S. Bucquet, registered by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer on 22 May 1936 and released on 23 May. Sieghardt’s filmography describes this film, produced by Jack Chertok and with Chic Sale, and credits its background music to Jurmann with Kaper, who were then under contract at MGM; no document of the time names a composer. His release date, 30 July 1936, and the “animated short” of Görner’s work list and the Jurmann catalogue belong to another Little Boy Blue of 1936, Ub Iwerks’s ComiColor cartoon released by Celebrity Productions that day. The archive recorded that cartoon here until November 2026.

Identified as the MGM Miniature by the copyright registration (SRC0983) and MGM’s release schedule (SRC0984), which match Sieghardt’s filmography (SRC0373, p. 226). The music credit rests on Sieghardt and the Jurmann works catalogue (SRC0306) alone and is probable. Iwerks’s cartoon of the same title (SRC0985) is not this film.
