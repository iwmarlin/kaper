Jazz Drops

Year
1930
Period
European · 1926–1934

About this work

StabiKat records nos. 1–5 under shelfmark DMS 266613. The surviving no. 6 score in the Bronislaw Kaper Papers preserves a series cover listing nos. 1–8, and Hofmeister independently confirms no. 7. A separately issued copy of no. 8 has not yet been located.
