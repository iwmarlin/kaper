Marcella Halicz — detail of the reception for Jan Kiepura, Paris, November 1933

Marcella Halicz, Jan Kiepura’s secretary and the author of the Polish lyrics of Ninon, ach uśmiechnij się! and Signorina, signorina, at the cocktail reception Kiepura gave in Paris in November 1933. Detail of the group photograph in which Kaper stands second from the left.
