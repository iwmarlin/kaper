Marc Lavry — portrait by Helmar Lerski, 1944

Marc Lavry photographed by Helmar Lerski in 1944, thirteen years after he directed the music for Seitensprünge (1931) in Berlin. Lavry conducted the Berliner Sinfonie Orchester from 1928 until the orchestra was dismantled under the National Socialist regime, and left for Riga in 1933.

Credit: Helmar Lerski, 1944; via the Marc Lavry Heritage Society and Wikimedia Commons.
