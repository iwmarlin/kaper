Pseudonyms and documented identities4

  • Bob Henders
    Joint printed composer identity resolved to Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann. The two prints of 1932 spell it differently: “Bob Henders” on Du liebst mich nicht mehr, “Bob Handers” on Eine Sommernacht am Meer.    Joint Pseudonym
  • Ernst Martin
    Hofmeister registers “Immer, wenn ich glücklich bin, muß ich schrecklich weinen” and “In 24 Stunden kann soviel gescheh’n” under the composer name “Ernst Martin.” The Buxbaum/Sieghardt catalogue attributes both songs to Walter Jurmann and Bronisław Kaper, identifying “Ernst Martin” as their joint printed identity in this context.    Joint Pseudonym
  • Pierre Candel
    Printed music credit on the four songs of Le chant du destin (1933) and on Le greluchon délicat (1934), where the name stands for Walter Jurmann alone. On Le bistrot du port and Rêves d’amour it stands for Kaper and Jurmann together.    Pseudonym
  • Pierre Candel
    Printed music credit on Le bistrot du port (1933) and Rêves d’amour (1934), where the name stands for Kaper and Jurmann together; the SACEM repertoire records both behind it. On the four songs of Le chant du destin and on Le greluchon délicat the same name stands for Jurmann alone.    Joint Pseudonym
