Siegfried Arno in Schatz, mach’ Kasse, 1926

Siegfried Arno in the Ama-Film comedy Schatz, mach’ Kasse (1926), in a photograph printed in the magazine Das Leben and cropped from a scene still with Ossi Oswalda. Five years later he sang the refrain of Kaper’s “Ach, Otto, Otto...!” with Marek Weber’s orchestra for Electrola.
