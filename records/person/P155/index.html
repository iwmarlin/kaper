Pseudonyms and documented identities1

  • Tihoti-Ré
    Tahitian pseudonym, given in the BnF person authority record as Tihoti-Ré or Tihoti-Re, with a see-also reference under that form. Tihoti is the Tahitian rendering of Georges. The Pathé disc credits the performing group as Tihoti-ré et son ensemble tahitien and names Tihoti in the vocal duo, so the composer credited as G. Rey and the singer credited as Tihoti are the same man.    Pseudonym

One 78 rpm disc, 25 cm, Pathé PA 1234, matrices CPT 3351 and CPT 3352. Catalogued by the Bibliothèque nationale de France with the language given as Tahitian. Accessed 1 August 2026.

  2. SRC0626 2026

    Bibliothèque nationale de France, notice de personne “Rey, Georges (1908-2011)”, forme internationale, FRBNF16974300. Born 27 May 1908 in Papeete, Tahiti; died 6 January 2011 in Bazas, Gironde. Described as a Tahitian composer, singer and musician who worked as an assistant book-keeper in an American camp in the Gironde, and known also under the pseudonym Tihoti-Ré, or Tihoti-Re. The notice cites as its sources the Pathé disc of “O chant d'amour de Tahiti !” and Claude Lestrade, “Quelques souvenirs de Tahiti de 1942 à 1945 (2e partie),” Journal de la Société des océanistes 95 (1992), pp. 251–274. Accessed 1 August 2026.
