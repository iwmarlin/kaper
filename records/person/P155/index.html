Pseudonyms and documented identities1

  • Tihoti-Ré
    Tahitian pseudonym, given in the BnF person authority record as Tihoti-Ré or Tihoti-Re, with a see-also reference under that form. Tihoti is the Tahitian rendering of Georges. The Pathé disc credits the performing group as Tihoti-ré et son ensemble tahitien and names Tihoti in the vocal duo, so the composer credited as G. Rey and the singer credited as Tihoti are the same man.    Pseudonym
