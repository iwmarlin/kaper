  3. SRC0828 2026

    Société des auteurs, compositeurs et éditeurs de musique. “Charles-Louis Pothier.” Musée SACEM, Les créateurs. Author, born at Bordeaux on 18 April 1881, died at Clichy on 3 November 1962; elected full member of SACEM on 17 February 1912; Prix Trebitsch, 1943; Prix Albert Willemetz, 1947; Prix Centenaire, 1950; Prix Célestin Joubert, 1956; Grand Prix de la Chanson, 1958. Accessed 25 August 2026.

Charles-Louis Pothier — portrait, Flossie programme, 1929

Charles-Louis Pothier, the Bordeaux-born lyricist who worked with René Pujol as librettist-parolier to Albert Willemetz at the Bouffes-Parisiens, in the author's portrait from the programme of Flossie.

Credit: Pierre Carles; programme of Flossie, Bouffes-Parisiens, 1929, collection Jacques Gana, via the Encyclopédie de la comédie musicale.
