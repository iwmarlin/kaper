  3. Marek Weber und sein Orchester. “Du lieber Geiger, spiel’ einen Tango für mich.” Electrola E.G. 2271, matrix OD267-1; recorded in the Electrola studio, Berlin, on 24 March 1931 and released in June 1931; tango from the film „Seitensprünge“; the reverse side is “Frühling muss es sein”. Composers given as Dr. Kaper und German, words by Fritz Rotter. The Electrola label list at musiktiteldb.de, following the Discographie der deutschen Tanzmusik, names the label, gives the matrix with its take and describes the side as instrumental, where the transfer's description called it a dance-orchestra recording with refrain vocal. Transfer published on the YouTube channel 78rpm Musik on 27 April 2025; the label is not shown. Accessed 17 August 2026.
