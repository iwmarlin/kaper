  6. SRC0616 2026

    Gana, Jacques. “MAUPREY (André).” Encyclopédie multimédia de la comédie musicale théâtrale en France. Gives the civil-status name André Jacques Bloch and the dates Paris, 19 August 1891 – Paris, 3 February 1939. The birth year conflicts with the Bibliothèque nationale de France and Wikidata, which both give 1881; day, month and date of death agree across all three sources, so the discrepancy is confined to one digit. The BnF-supported year 1881 is preferred; the conflict remains unresolved. Accessed 1 August 2026.
