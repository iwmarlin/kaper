  1. SRC0204 1930

    Kaper, B. "Sagen kleine Mädels \"nein\"." In Der Korvettenkapitän (Blaue Jungs von der Marine), Tonfilm: Schlagheft für Gesang mit Pianoforte. Berlin: Alrobi, 1930. Listed in Hofmeisters Musikalisch-literarischer Monatsbericht 1930 as English waltz; also paired with "Was braucht denn jede Frau leider unbedingt?" for Salonorchester with jazz instruments, arranged by F. Stolzenwald.

  2. SRC0471 1930

    U.S. Copyright Office, Catalogue of Copyright Entries, New Series. Part 3: Musical Compositions, vol. 25, nos. 1–12, for the year 1930. Washington, DC: U.S. Government Printing Office, 1931, entry no. 29429 for “Der Korvettenkapitän”: Schlager from the film; text by Fritz Rotter and Armin Robinson; music by B. Kaper, of Poland. Includes “Blaue Jung’s von der Marine” and three other items. © July 25, 1930; 1 copy received November 10, 1930; E for. 14747; Alrobi Musikverlag G.m.b.H., Berlin. Digitized via Internet Archive.
