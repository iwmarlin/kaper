  4. SRC0700 1933

    Hans Albers, with the Odeon-Künstler-Orchester conducted by O. Dobrindt. “Ich bin der Hans im Glück” / “In 24 Stunden.” Odeon O-11 810, shellac, 10-inch, 78 rpm, Germany, 1933. Side B, “In 24 Stunden,” is described as a tango from the Boston-Tonfilm „Heut’ kommt’s drauf an“, with music by B. Kaper and Walter Jurmann and words by Fritz Rotter; matrix Be 10225, recorded in Berlin on 9 February 1933, side A carrying matrix Be 10224 from the same session. Discogs, release 28872088. Accessed 15 August 2026.

  5. SRC0697 n.d.

Hans Albers in Berlin, 1924

Hans Albers photographed walking Unter den Linden in Berlin, 1924 — nine years before he took the lead in Heut' kommt's drauf an, the 1933 comedy for which Kaper and Jurmann wrote the songs.
