Franz Waxman — Musical Courier, July 1955 (detail)

Franz Waxman, documented in connection with Trouble for Two (1936), A Day at the Races (1937) and The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn (1939), rehearses with Les Brown’s Band of Renown for the opening concert of the Los Angeles Music Festival in June 1955, which Waxman conducted.
