Sources linked directly to this person4

  1. SRC0716 1930

    Saxophon Orchester Dobbri, mit Gesang: Max Mensing. “Was macht jede Nacht so ein kleines Baby in der Bar.” Slow-Fox; label credit “Fritz Rotter & Dr. B. Kaper”. Parlophone, Katalog-Nr. 80.235, matrix B 38451, n.d. The label carries the B.I.E.M. mark; the uploader dates the recording to Berlin, 1930, and describes this pressing as a French Parlophon issue. Shellac disc from the Schallarchiv of the Radiomuseum Hardthausen, its label filmed in the YouTube upload.

  2. SRC0699 1933

    Hans Albers mit Odeon-Künstler-Orchester, Leitung: Kapellmeister O. Dobrindt. “Immer wenn ich glücklich bin.” Foxtrot a. d. Boston-Tonfilm „Heut’ kommt’s drauf an“; label credits “Musik: Walter Jurmann u. B. Kaper” and “Text: Fritz Rotter”. Odeon O-11811 a, matrix Be 10226, 78 R, B.I.E.M.; the label carries a dealer sticker of C. A. Klemm, Musik- und Pianohaus, Chemnitz, Brüderstr. 2–4, and the notice “Radiosendung ohne Genehmigung verboten”. Label filmed in the YouTube upload by Schellackplatte, uploaded 21 May 2008. Accessed 15 August 2026.

  3. SRC0700 1933

    Hans Albers, with the Odeon-Künstler-Orchester conducted by O. Dobrindt. “Ich bin der Hans im Glück” / “In 24 Stunden.” Odeon O-11 810, shellac, 10-inch, 78 rpm, Germany, 1933. Side B, “In 24 Stunden,” is described as a tango from the Boston-Tonfilm „Heut’ kommt’s drauf an“, with music by B. Kaper and Walter Jurmann and words by Fritz Rotter; matrix Be 10225, recorded in Berlin on 9 February 1933, side A carrying matrix Be 10224 from the same session. Discogs, release 28872088. Accessed 15 August 2026.

    Open source ↗

  4. SRC0697 n.d.

    Hans Albers, with the Odeon-Künstler-Orchester under Otto Dobrindt. “Mein Gorilla hat 'ne Villa im Zoo.” Paso Doble; Odeon, Katalog-Nr. O-11811, n.d. The description gives the matrix number Be 10227. Discographic detail as given in the uploader's description; the label is not shown in the YouTube upload. The companion side, Odeon O-11811 a with matrix Be 10226, survives with its label filmed, which corroborates the catalogue number and places this recording as the reverse of the same disc.
