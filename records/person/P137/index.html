Deanna Durbin — Cine Mundial cover portrait, February 1938

Deanna Durbin on the cover of Cine Mundial, February 1938 — a painted portrait by Jose M. Recoder, not a photograph. Durbin sang “My heart is singing” and “Someone to care for me”, written for her by Kaper and Jurmann for Three Smart Girls (1936).

Credit: Jose M. Recoder, Cine Mundial, February 1938, via the Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons.
