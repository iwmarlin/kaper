  1. SRC0707 1929

    Marek Weber und sein Orchester, Refraingesang: Austin Egen. “Tränen weint jede Frau so gern.” Tango; His Master's Voice (Electrola), 1929. The description gives the year as 1929, names Nico Dostal as arranger, and credits the tango to Dr. Bronislaw Kaper with words by Fritz Rotter. Discographic detail as given in the uploader's description; the label is not shown in the YouTube upload.
