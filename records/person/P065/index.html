  3. SRC0613 2026

    Gana, Jacques. “PUJOL (René).” Encyclopédie multimédia de la comédie musicale théâtrale en France. Gives the civil-status name Amédée Ferdinand Pujol and the dates Bordeaux, 15 May 1878 – Paris, 21 January 1942, together with a list of stage works and press articles. Accessed 1 August 2026. The birth year conflicts across authority sources: the Library of Congress record no2009029783 gives 1888 and GND 1062158474 gives 18 August 1888, while VIAF 44567556 and this encyclopedia give 1878, the latter with the precise date 15 May. All sources agree on the death date, 21 January 1942. The preferred birth year is 1878; the discrepancy remains unresolved.

    Open source ↗
