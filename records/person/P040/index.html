Pseudonyms and documented identities1

  • Jean Cis
    Jean Cis is a pseudonym of Francis Salabert. The Paris print of “Ça va déjà bien mieux !” carries Jean Cis as the printed credit for the words, and the Bibliothèque nationale de France enters that print under Salabert as author of the words.    Pseudonym
