  • Bruno Asper

  • Harry Morell
    The printed composer name “Harry Morell” is identified as Bronisław Kaper on “Wer weiß, ob du mich auch liebst”.    Pseudonym
  • Bob Henders
    Joint printed composer identity resolved to Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann.    Joint Pseudonym
  • Bob Handers
    Joint printed composer identity resolved to Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann.    Joint Pseudonym
  • Ernst Martin
    Hofmeister registers “Immer, wenn ich glücklich bin, muß ich schrecklich weinen” and “In 24 Stunden kann soviel gescheh'n” under the composer name “Ernst Martin.” The Buxbaum/Sieghardt catalogue attributes both songs to Walter Jurmann and Bronisław Kaper, identifying “Ernst Martin” as their joint printed identity in this context.    Joint Pseudonym
  • Pierre Candel
    On “Le bistrot du port” and “Rêves d'amour”, the printed name Pierre Candel is identified jointly with Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann.    Joint Pseudonym
  • Henryk Chwast
    Registration and catalogue identity resolved to Bronisław Kaper; distinct from the printed Bruno Asper credits.    Registration Identity
