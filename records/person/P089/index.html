Alexander Zakin — studio portrait by Max Dupain, July 1947

Alexander Zakin performed in the 1929 Vierflügeltango for four grand pianos and in the ERKLA-Quartett concert at Hamborn in December 1929, where he played alongside Kaper. Max Dupain made this later portrait in July 1947.

Credit: Max Dupain, July 1947; Mitchell Library, State Library of New South Wales, ON 558/Box 13/no. 90; via Wikimedia Commons.
