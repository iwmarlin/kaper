Louis Poterat — Les Ondes, 20 July 1941

Louis Poterat, who wrote the French lyrics of Le plus petit baiser and À Paris tiguidiguidi in 1933 and of Amanda and C’est ton amour in 1934. The portrait is later: it announced a broadcast in the Paris radio weekly Les Ondes on 20 July 1941.

Credit: Photographer unnamed; Les Ondes (Paris), 20 July 1941; digitised issue at World Radio History.
