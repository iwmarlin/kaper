  2. SRC0710 1931

    Marek Weber und sein Orchester, Refrain: Leo Moll. “Signorina aus Brioni.” Tango; label credit “Tango (Fritz Rotter und Dr. Kaper); Text: Fritz Rotter und Dr. Kaper”. Electrola, Katalog-Nr. E.G. 2400, n.d. The label also carries the B.I.E.M. mark and the designation Tanz-Orchester mit Refraingesang; the uploader identifies the refrain singer billed as Leo Moll as Leo Monosson, gives 1931 as the year of release, and illustrates the transfer with a photograph of Brioni from 1930. Shellac disc from the Schallarchiv of the Radiomuseum Hardthausen, its label filmed in the YouTube upload.
