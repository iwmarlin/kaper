Albert Chantrier and René Nazelles — La Rampe, 1921

Albert Chantrier and René Nazelles, composer and lyricist of Le Fruit défendu, photographed for La Rampe on the operetta's run at the Théâtre Cluny. Nazelles went on to write French lyrics for Kaper.
