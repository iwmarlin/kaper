  2. SRC0628 2026

    Deutsche Nationalbibliothek, GND 1061685829, “Péguy, Robert”, director, born 14 December 1883 and died 21 July 1968, with the variant forms “Péguy, Marcel Robert” and “Robert, Marcel”. The Library of Congress heading no2009019373 reads “Péguy, Robert, 1883-1968”. The French Wikipedia article gives the birth name as Marcel Robert Péguy and both the birth and the death in the 17th arrondissement of Paris. Neither authority file records the spelling Pégy; the GND links directly to the filmportal.de record for the same man, so the form printed in the credits of Son altesse l'amour is a transcription of that one film rather than a name he used. Accessed 1 August 2026.
