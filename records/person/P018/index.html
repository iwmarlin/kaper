Frank Lloyd with his Best Director award, April 1930

Frank Lloyd at the second Academy Awards ceremony on 3 April 1930, holding the Best Director award for The Divine Lady. Five years later he directed Mutiny on the Bounty, for which Kaper wrote “Love Song of Tahiti” with Walter Jurmann and Gus Kahn.
