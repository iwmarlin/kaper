Marc-Cab — portrait, Encyclopédie de la comédie musicale

Marc-Cab — Marcel Cabridens, born in Nice in 1901 and died at Bandol in 1978 — who wrote the French lyrics of Vivons l'amour! Vivons la vie, C'est chez nous and Un baiser de femme sous un ciel de feu in 1934 and of Celui que j'aime in 1939. The photograph is undated.
