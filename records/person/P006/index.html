André de Badet — portrait, Gaîté-Lyrique programme, 1941

André de Badet, the librettist and lyricist who wrote French words for seven Kaper songs, in the author’s portrait printed in the programme of his operetta L’Auberge qui chante at the Gaîté-Lyrique.
