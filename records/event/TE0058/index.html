First performance of a work by Kaper at the Bach-Saal

Concert LifeEuropean
Date
3 December 1927
Period

Precision

Category
Concert Life

Event

Róża Etkin had already played works by Kaper in Berlin in the spring of 1927: Signale für die musikalische Welt (no. 20) reviewed a recital devoted partly to Chopin and partly to newer Polish composers, naming Szymanowski and B. Kaper but not his works. On 3 December 1927 she gave a recital at the Bach-Saal whose programme — four Chopin ballades and works by Karol Szymanowski, Maurice Ravel and Isaac Albéniz, with Liszt’s Sonata in B minor — included the first performance of a work by B. Kaper. The announcement does not name the work. Kaper later recalled writing a piano sonatina for Etkin, which she played in Berlin; the Sonatina manuscript is dated 1927, but no source ties it to either recital.
