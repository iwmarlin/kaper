Timeline event · TE0028

Migration from Berlin via Vienna to Paris

Date
1933
Period
European · 1926–1934
Place
Berlin; Paris
Category
migration

Summary

Following Nazi persecution, Kaper and Jurmann flee Berlin after UFA terminates Jewish employees' contracts. Decisive moment: Kaper and his wife are attacked by Nazi gang on main Berlin street. Both composers relocate to Paris.

Sources

  1. Lindstedt, “Between Migration and Exile” (2026/forthcoming)
