Born on January 23 (February 5, New Style) in Warsaw. Father – Szmul Moszek (later Stanisław/Samuel) Kaper, bookkeeper, aged 28. Mother – Maria (Mariam) née Mucha, aged 24. The family is of Jewish faith (registered at the Office of Civil Status for Non-Christian Confessions, 8th Jerusalem District, Warsaw).
