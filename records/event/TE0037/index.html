Summary

First American score: "Escapade" (dir. Robert Z. Leonard) with William Powell and Luise Rainer. Press hails "Two New Composers" of Kiepura's European hits. Songs also for Marx Brothers' "A Night at the Opera" (with Ned Washington) and "Mutiny on the Bounty".
