Event

Kaper contributed to Escapade, directed by Robert Z. Leonard and starring William Powell and Luise Rainer. For the film, which opened in July 1935, Kaper and Jurmann supplied a single number, “You’re All I Need”, to words by Gus Kahn; the AFI catalogue adds Harold Adamson. He also contributed songs to the Marx Brothers’ A Night at the Opera, with Ned Washington, and to Mutiny on the Bounty. MGM engaged him at first as a song specialist rather than as a composer of film scores; by his own account, he moved between those roles only gradually. The press introduced Kaper and Jurmann as “Two New Composers” of Kiepura’s European hits.
