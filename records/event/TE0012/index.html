Event

On February 14, passes the judicial exam (egzamin prawno-sądowy) — the final of three required examinations — with a grade of "satisfactory" (dostateczny). On March 14, the Faculty of Law issues diploma no. 124. On May 26, receives his Certificate of Departure (Świadectwo Wystąpienia) no. 944.
