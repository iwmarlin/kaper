Cocktail with Jan Kiepura

Professional NetworkEuropean

Event

In February, Kaper attends a cocktail reception for Jan Kiepura at the George V in Paris together with Walter Jurmann. A contemporary press report names them among the invited guests and identifies them as the composers of the music for Tout pour l’amour.
