Founding member of the European Film Fund (EFF), established by Paul Kohner, Ernst Lubitsch, and Charlotte Dieterle to aid refugees arriving from Europe. Despite his relatively secure position at MGM, Kaper joined the inner circle of organizers during the critical surge of refugee arrivals in 1938–1941.
