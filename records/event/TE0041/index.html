Event

The European Film Fund was established in 1938 by Paul Kohner, Ernst Lubitsch, and Carl Laemmle to aid refugees arriving from Europe. Kaper participated in the Fund despite his relatively secure position at MGM. A surviving donor list for 1 May 1941–30 April 1942 records “Kaper, Bronislaw” and a contribution of $120, providing direct documentary evidence of his involvement.
