Bick & Kaper at Berlin premiere

Date
1929
Period
European · 1926–1934
Place
Berlin
Category
performance

Summary

The jazz piano duo Bick und Kaper performs at the Ufa-Theater "Universum" in Berlin, opening the premiere of "Der Adjutant des Zaren." The Rhein- und Ruhrzeitung writes: "Their technique is astonishing."

Sources
