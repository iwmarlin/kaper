Public performance at the Chopin Music School concert

Event

In June, appears at a public concert of the Fryderyk Chopin Higher School of Music at the Warsaw Philharmonic as a pupil of Józef Śmidowicz’s piano class. Performs the first movement of Chopin’s Piano Concerto in F minor. Warsaw critics note his “light finger technique” and “elegance combined with concentrated lyricism,”

  2. SRC0816 2023

    Józef Kański. “Śmidowicz, Józef.” Encyklopedia, Polska Biblioteka Muzyczna, Polskie Wydawnictwo Muzyczne. Entry for the pianist and teacher (Zhytomyr, 2 September 1888 – Warsaw, 10 June 1962): pupil of Aleksander Michałowski at the Warsaw Music Institute, 1905–13; professor of piano at the school of the Warsaw Music Society from 1918, from 1919 the Fryderyk Chopin Higher School of Music, a post he held until 1950; professor at the State Higher School of Music in Warsaw from 1951 and dean of its piano faculty 1953–59; juror of the first three International Chopin Competitions.
