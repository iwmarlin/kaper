Entry into Warsaw popular song: Craig, Włast, and Altschuler

Event

Kaper’s own account presents the move into songwriting as accidental: a friend whom he called “Henry Craig” introduced him to a leading Warsaw lyricist identifiable as Andrzej Włast, after which the song reached the cabaret stage and then print. Read together with the surviving printed songs, this episode explains how a composer trained in concert idioms entered the commercial structures of interwar Warsaw popular song: lyricist, cabaret performer, publisher, and sheet-music circulation.
