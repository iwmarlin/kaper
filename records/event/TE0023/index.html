Event

Dr. Funk’s review “Jazz auf vier Flügeln” in the Rhein- und Ruhrzeitung, evening edition, 10 December 1929, p. 2, documents an ERKLA concert in Hamborn. It names Kaper alongside Giuliano Pomeranz, then performing as Julian Pomeranc, Alexander Zakin, and Leopold Mittmann. Kaper joined the ensemble after Adam Gelbtrunk’s departure, at Leopold Mittmann’s urging; the announcements of the late 1920s otherwise list the quartet without him, so the association cannot be counted as a regular one. Dr. Funk credited him with a “Vierflügeltango” of sonorous linear writing and Mittmann with a “Jazz-Prélude” and three “Jazz-Babies”.
