ERKLA-Quartett concert review in Hamborn

Date
10 December 1929
Period
European · 1926–1934
Place
Hamborn (Duisburg), Germany
Category
concert_life

Summary

Dr. Funk’s review “Jazz auf vier Flügeln” in the Rhein- und Ruhrzeitung, 10 December 1929, p. 12, documents an ERKLA concert in Hamborn. It names Kaper alongside Giuliano Pomeranz, then performing as Julian Pomeranc, Aleksander Zakin, and Leopold Mittmann.

Sources
