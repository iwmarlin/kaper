End of the Jurmann–Kaper partnership

Event

The last work registered in the names of both composers is “At night”, a cue for The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn written with Franz Waxman and registered in the United States on 25 February 1939. Sieghardt’s catalogue and Görner’s work list name two later songs by the pair, “Harbour Café”, published by Criterion in 1941, and “Blush and Sigh”, written for When Ladies Meet (1941) and not used; no document of the time confirms either. No document in the archive gives the reasons the partnership ended.
