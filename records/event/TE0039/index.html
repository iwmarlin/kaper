Event

On 4 February 1936, Bronisław Kaper and Eleonora Kaper each filed a Declaration of Intention in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of California, Los Angeles. Bronisław Kaper’s declaration records him as residing at Château des Fleurs, 6626 Franklin Ave., Hollywood.
