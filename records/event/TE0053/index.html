MGM markets Kaper and Jurmann as “Viennese composers”

Reception

Event

Soon after their arrival at MGM, the American press and studio publicity presented Kaper and Jurmann as “Viennese composers” and as a brilliant Viennese musical team. The label suited MGM’s first use of the duo in Escapade, a Hollywood remake of the Viennese film Maskerade, but it was biographically misleading for Kaper, whose career had developed in Warsaw and Berlin. The event marks the moment when Hollywood began to translate his European professional history into a marketable studio identity.
