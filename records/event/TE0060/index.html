Gwiazdy Warszawy premieres at Morskie Oko

Event

While Kaper was living in Berlin, his song reached the Warsaw stage: the revue Gwiazdy Warszawy opened at Morskie Oko on 30 January 1930, announced in Kurjer Warszawski four days earlier. The printed edition names the number as the popular refrain from Morskie Oko in that revue — “Popularny refren z „Morskiego Oka” w rewji „Gwiazdy Warszawy”” — with words by Andrzej Włast and music by Bronisław Kaper, published by Gebethner i Wolff. On 1 February Tygodnik Ilustrowany advertised five dance numbers from the revue, this among them, and Tadeusz Faliszewski recorded it for Syrena-Electro in March. Neither the announcement nor the sheet names the rest of the programme.
