Application to Faculty of Philosophy

Event

In September, applies for transfer to the Faculty of Philosophy, Humanities Section. The immatriculation committee conditionally accepts him, pending a certificate of departure from the Faculty of Law. Takes academic leave for the winter and summer semesters of 1923/24.
