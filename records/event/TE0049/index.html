Alraune as Kaper’s first documented sound-film commission

Kaper’s first documented sound-film work belongs to the early phase of cinema in which the film composer’s task was still closely tied to song production. Alraune, Richard Oswald’s 1930 sound remake, did not yet require the later Hollywood model of continuous underscoring. Instead, Kaper contributed songs that could serve the film while also entering the wider economy of printed scores, records, performers, and radio.
