Serves as a volunteer in the Polish-Soviet War in the rank of Senior Private (starszy szeregowy) in the Reserve Depot (Skadrowany Zapasowy) of the 1st Reconnaissance Aviation Regiment (1. Pułk Lotniczy Wywiadowczy), stationed at Mokotów, Warsaw. On November 5, he is granted indefinite leave as an "academic volunteer" (akademik ochotnik), allowing him to resume his university studies.
