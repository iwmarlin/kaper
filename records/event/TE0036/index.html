Event

Enters for permanent residence at Calexico, California, from Mexicali on 1 November 1935, travelling on foot with Eleonora. The manifest records 6626 Franklin Ave., Hollywood, as his U.S. destination; his Declaration of Intention four months later records the same address as his residence.
