While the composers head to America, their European films and songs continue to circulate. In September 1934, "Madame wird kinderlieb" still plays in Stuttgart with Jurmann and Kaper credited — despite Nazi purges. In France, "Tout pour l’amour" with Kiepura screens as late as 1936; the Journal de Vichy calls their music "infiniment plaisante."
