Everybody Sing with Gus Kahn

Event

On 13 July 1937, The Washington Daily News reported that Gus Kahn was studying musical notation with Kaper and Jurmann while they worked on The Ugly Duckling, the working title of Everybody Sing. A Brooklyn Daily Eagle notice of 17 March 1938 documents the released film and credits songs to Kaper and Jurmann.
