Summary

The manuscript credit for Hätt’ ich einen Kammerdiener places Kaper beside Fritz Rotter at an early point in his Berlin years. It is an important transitional event: Rotter linked Kaper to the practical economy of the German hit song—publishers, performers, promotion, and sheet-music…
