Event

The manuscript credit for Hätt’ ich einen Kammerdiener places Kaper beside Fritz Rotter at an early point in his Berlin years. It is an important transitional event: Rotter linked Kaper to the practical economy of the German hit song—publishers, performers, promotion, and sheet-music circulation—before those skills were absorbed into the machinery of early sound film. It also explains why Rotter later became the mediator who brought Kaper and Walter Jurmann together.
