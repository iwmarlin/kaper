Duke Ellington and His Famous Orchestra recorded “All God’s Chillun Got Rhythm” in New York on 8 June 1937 for Master (MA 137, matrix M 519-1). Written by Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann with words by Gus Kahn for A Day at the Races, the song passed from the screen into the jazz repertoire within weeks of the film’s release.
