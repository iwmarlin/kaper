Multiple-language versions and transnational sound-film production

Event

The practice of producing parallel language versions was one of the key industrial conditions of early sound cinema. It allowed music to move more easily than dialogue across national markets, even when lyrics, singers, and performance contexts changed. Kaper and Jurmann’s work in this system gave their songs and professional skills a transnational character before their forced departure from Germany.
