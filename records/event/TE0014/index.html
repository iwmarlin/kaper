Earliest published cabaret songs

Warsaw MusicWarsaw

Event

Songs for Warsaw cabarets published under the pseudonym Bruno Asper, to words by Andrzej Włast. The printed editions of 1926 are the earliest published songs documented here; the signature itself is older, appearing on the 1923 manuscript of Koszutski Girls.
