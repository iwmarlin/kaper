Together with pianist Hermann Biek, credited on recordings as Hermann Bick, Kaper records two-piano arrangements for Deutsche Grammophon/Polydor. The documented repertoire includes American popular songs such as “Miss Annabelle Lee” and “The Song Is Ended.”
