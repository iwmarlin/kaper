Summary

Along with Hermann Bick (Bieck), he records piano duo arrangements for Deutsche Grammophon/Polydor. These sessions showcase contemporary American jazz standards including "Miss Annabelle Lee," "The Varsity Drag," and "The Song Is Ended." The collaboration blends classical virtuosity with jazz…
