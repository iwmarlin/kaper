Ufa resolves to terminate the contracts of its Jewish staff

Film CareerEuropean
Date
29 March 1933
Period
European · 1926–1934
Precision
Day
Category
Film Career

Event

On 28 March 1933 Joseph Goebbels addressed representatives of the film industry at the Hotel Kaiserhof in Berlin. The next day the board of Universum-Film AG resolved in principle that contracts with its Jewish employees were to be terminated wherever possible, each board member deciding who in his department was to go at once and who was to be removed “im Wege eines langsamen Abbaues”, through a gradual reduction. The resolution was not limited to German citizens, so it reached Kaper and Jurmann as well, and its effect on their work was visible during the production of Kind, ich freu’ mich auf Dein Kommen.
