The Sängerfilm and the star-singer circuit

Event

The Kaper–Jurmann partnership developed inside the commercial ecology of early European musical film. Their songs for productions associated with singers such as Richard Tauber and Jan Kiepura were not simply film numbers: they were designed to travel beyond the screen as printed music, recordings, radio items, and repertory for performers. This star-singer circuit explains why the duo’s European experience later appeared useful to MGM, which read it through the promotional language of Viennese musical competence.
