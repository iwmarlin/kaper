The certificate of good conduct issued by the Warsaw State Police forms an important documentary link between Kaper’s university records and the later baptismal entry. It anchors the discussion of identity in administrative sources rather than retrospectively imposed narratives: Kaper appears in the record as a young Warsaw student from an assimilated Jewish family whose formal confessional status changed only a few months later.
