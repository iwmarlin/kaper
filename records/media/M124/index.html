About this item

Sworn immigration manifest (U. S. Department of Labor, INS Form 548) for Bronisław Kaper at the Port of Calexico, California, recording his admission for permanent residence as a 'music composer' (born Warsaw, Poland; wife Eleonora Kaper). Part of Kaper's U. S. immigration record.
