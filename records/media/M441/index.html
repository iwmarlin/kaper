About this item

Nacio Herb Brown at a piano in a promotional photograph with Anita Page for The Hollywood Revue of 1929, published in Photoplay in October 1929. The local portrait is cropped from the full source image. Brown’s songs were among the contemporary American repertory arranged by Kaper in Berlin.
