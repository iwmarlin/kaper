Hamborn, Rathaus — postcard posted in 1920

Fit to window

The town hall at Hamborn, on a postcard posted there in October 1920.

About this item

Picture postcard “Hamborn. Partie am Rathaus”, showing the town hall of Hamborn with its clock tower. The card was written and postmarked at Hamborn on 7 October 1920 and carries a 30-pfennig Germania stamp. Hamborn was then an independent town; it was joined to Duisburg in 1929.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Postcard, publisher and photographer unidentified; Zeno.org; via Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain. The card names no photographer and was published by 1920, when it was posted at Hamborn; the German term for an anonymous work, seventy years from publication, had therefore run out before 1 January 1996, so the URAA restored nothing, and a work published before 1931 is in any case out of copyright in the United States.
