About this item

Kaper-Jurmann’s first American song, written for William Powell’s Viennese comedy at MGM. Ranked No. 14 on the ASCAP list of most-played songs of 1935–36, with over 21,000 radio broadcasts on NBC and CBS — placing the newcomers alongside Irving Berlin and Jerome Kern.
