About this item

Newspaper notice issued by the acting director of A. M. Skrynnikowa’s Warsaw seven-grade boys’ gymnasium, announcing entrance and resit examinations at the school’s building at Żórawia 49. Published in 1914, during Kaper’s documented period of study at the school.
