About this item

The printed caption identifies the two men by what they are holding rather than by position: “FRANZ WAXMAN (with saxophone), music director and conductor of the 1955 Los Angeles Music Festival, rehearses with Les Brown for the opening night concert of the Festival on June 6. Waxman conducted Brown's Band of Renown and the Festival Symphony Orchestra in the West Coast premiere of Liebermann's Concerto for Jazz Band and Symphony Orchestra. ” The reproduced detail shows the man holding the saxophone. The photograph is credited “Photo: Dave Pell. ”

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photograph credited to Dave Pell; Musical Courier, vol. 152, no. 1 (July 1955), p. 27; Summy-Birchard Publishing Company; scanned copy at the Internet Archive.

Public domain in the United States. The photograph was published in Musical Courier in July 1955, when copyright required renewal after twenty-eight years. The Online Books Page inventory compiled from the Catalog of Copyright Entries and Copyright Office records lists May 1961 as the first renewed issue of the title, indicating that the 1955 issue copyright was not renewed and expired on 1 January 1984. The inventory notes that some individual contributions were renewed separately; no separate registration has been identified for this photograph, which carries only a caption credit. The displayed image is a 340 × 491 px detail cropped to Waxman.
