About this item

Official 1928 aerial mosaic showing the eastern end of Pommersche Straße at Sächsische Straße in Berlin-Wilmersdorf. Kaper’s 1932 address book entry gives Pommersche Straße 3; the historical parcel is identified from the later official plan for Sächsische Straße 48–49 / Pommersche Straße 3–4.
