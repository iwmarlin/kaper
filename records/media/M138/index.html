About this item

Review titled ‘Jazz auf vier Flügeln — Zweites städtisches Sonderkonzert in Hamborn’ (signed Dr. Funk) in the Rhein- und Ruhr-Zeitung, 10 December 1929. It covers the First Piano Quartet (Erstes Klavier-Quartett) of four pianists — Bronisław Kaper, Leopold Mittmann, Alexander Zakin and Julian Pomeranc — performing on four grand pianos, including their own compositions. The programme ranged from Schubert, Verdi, Strauss, Kreisler and Rimsky-Korsakov to the ‘Blue Danube’ and modern dances; Kaper contributed a tango for four grand pianos (Vierflügeltango), and Mittmann a Jazz-Prélude and three ‘Jazz-Babies’.
