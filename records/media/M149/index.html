About this item

Cover of Illustrierter Film-Kurier, no. 1946, devoted to Heut’ kommt’s drauf an (1933), Kurt Gerron’s film with Hans Albers and music by Walter Jurmann and Bronisław Kaper. This is a film-program/periodical cover, not a standard theatrical poster.
