About this item

Early sound film directed by Richard Oswald, featuring music by Bronisław Kaper, including the tango “Wenn mich Männer betrügen” performed by Brigitte Helm. The film's opening (first 5 minutes) is available on filmportal. de, digitized in 2021 as part of the Förderprogramm Filmerbe (FFE). Click above to watch on the original source.
