About this item

Detail cropped from the lower of two photographs on page 11 of Die Bühne, number 6 of 1940, reporting a press reception at which Erich von Neusser, deputy head of production of Wien-Film, spoke about film-making in Vienna. The full photograph shows, from left to right, von Neusser, the actress Hilde Krahl and the director Gustav Ucicky; the detail is confined to von Neusser, seated with a cigarette over his papers.
