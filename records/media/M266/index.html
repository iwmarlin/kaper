Henry Koster — Universal-International press photograph, 1961

Fit to window

Henry Koster, who directed Three Smart Girls in 1936, the Deanna Durbin debut that carried two songs by Kaper and Jurmann. The photograph is a later studio still, made for Flower Drum Song in 1961.
