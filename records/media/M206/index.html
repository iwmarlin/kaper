About this item

Early Polish sheet music for “Ja nie mam głosu! ”, a foxtrot with words by Andrzej Włast and music credited on the print to Bruno Asper, the pseudonym used by Bronisław Kaper in his early Warsaw publications. Published by Gebethner i Wolff, Warsaw, 1927; copy held by the National Library of Poland / Polona. This record presents the cover and first page of the same edition.
