Judy Garland — publicity photograph, 1936

Fit to window

Judy Garland in a studio publicity photograph issued for Pigskin Parade (1936), her feature debut. The following year she sang “Swing, Mister Mendelssohn”, by Kaper and Jurmann, in Everybody Sing (MGM, 1938).

Rights and provenance

Credit: 20th Century Fox, via Heritage Auctions and Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain in the United States: published between 1931 and 1977 without a copyright notice, per the Wikimedia Commons file. Two details are recorded rather than smoothed over: the Heritage Auctions lot the file was taken from is described as an MGM group of the 1940s, while the uploader dates this print to 1936 and attributes it to 20th Century Fox, which matches Pigskin Parade; and the Commons copy has been digitally cleaned.
