About this item

Interior view of the Société des Cinéromans studios at Joinville-le-Pont, showing the steel roof structure, suspended projectors and overhead lighting equipment. The photograph accompanied Jacques Lefebvre’s industrial report on the Joinville complex in Pour Vous in January 1929.
