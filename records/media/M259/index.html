Jeanette MacDonald — studio portrait, 1930s

Fit to window

Jeanette MacDonald in an undated studio portrait of the 1930s. MacDonald introduced “San Francisco”, by Kaper and Jurmann with words by Gus Kahn, in the MGM film of 1936; the song carried the picture and outlived it.
