About this item

Oval studio portrait of the director Robert Péguy, printed in blue ink beside his signed statement on the French film decree of 1928; the type of the reverse shows through the page.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer uncredited; La Critique cinématographique, 3 March 1928, p. 8. Source gallica.bnf.fr / Bibliothèque nationale de France.

Public domain. The digitising library marks the issue as public domain, and the photograph is printed without a photographer’s or agency’s credit, so the seventy-year term for an anonymous work published in 1928 ran out at the end of 1998.
