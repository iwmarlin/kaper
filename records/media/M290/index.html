Public domain. The photograph was taken on 1 August 1944 by an official Royal Navy photographer and is Crown copyright. Crown copyright in photographs created before 1 June 1957 ran for fifty years from creation, so the image entered the public domain in the United Kingdom in 1995 and was therefore already free on the United States restoration date of 1 January 1996, which leaves it unrestored by the URAA. Reproduced from the cropped detail published on Wikimedia Commons; the full frame shows Coward at the microphone.
