About this item

Inscribed portrait of the director Maurice Elvey, published in the British film weekly Pictures and the Picturegoer in June 1915. Elvey directed My Song for You, the English version of Ein Lied für dich, for Gaumont British in 1934.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Pictures and the Picturegoer, 5 June 1915. Wikimedia Commons, public domain.

Public domain as published, following the Wikimedia Commons file: printed in an American-distributed British weekly in June 1915, long before the threshold from which copyright still runs. The object reproduced is a photographic print rather than a page of that weekly, and no photographer is named.
