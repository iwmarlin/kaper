Ernst Lubitsch in Hollywood, 1930, photographed by Erich Salomon. Lubitsch was among the founders of the European Film Fund.

About this item

Ernst Lubitsch at his desk in Hollywood, taking papers from his secretary, photographed by Erich Salomon in 1930. Lubitsch was one of the founders of the European Film Fund in 1938.
