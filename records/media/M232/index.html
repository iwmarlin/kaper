Zula Pogorzelska in My chcemy króla at Teatr Perskie Oko (1926)

Fit to window

Zula Pogorzelska in a scene from “My chcemy króla” at Teatr “Perskie Oko,” Warsaw, April 1926. The photograph documents the visual culture of the Warsaw revue stage during Kaper’s early professional period.

About this item

Stage photograph of Zula Pogorzelska in a scene from the revue “My chcemy króla” at Warsaw’s Teatr “Perskie Oko,” April 1926. The photograph provides visual context for the interwar revue environment in which Kaper’s earliest Warsaw songs and professional networks developed; it is not used as evidence for a Kaper credit in this revue.
