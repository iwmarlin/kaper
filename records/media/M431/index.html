About this item

Press portrait of the lyricist Léo Lelièvre fils, printed in the Paris daily L’Ami du peuple on 12 May 1929 with the studio credit “(Photo Henri Manuel)”.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photo Henri Manuel; L’Ami du peuple, 12 May 1929, p. 9. RetroNews, Bibliothèque nationale de France.

Public domain. The portrait carries the studio credit of Henri Manuel, who died in 1947, so the seventy-year term of protection in France ran out at the end of 2017; the issue in which it appeared was published in 1929.
