Marc Lavry — portrait by Helmar Lerski, 1944

Fit to window

Marc Lavry photographed by Helmar Lerski in 1944, thirteen years after he directed the music for Seitensprünge (1931) in Berlin. Lavry conducted the Berliner Sinfonie Orchester from 1928 until the orchestra was dismantled under the National Socialist regime, and left for Riga in 1933.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Helmar Lerski, 1944; via the Marc Lavry Heritage Society and Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain in Israel: the Wikimedia Commons file carries the PD-Israel tag, under which copyright in a photograph made before 2008 expires fifty years after it was taken, so the term ended in 1994. That ground is Israeli, not United States law; Lerski died in 1956, so the work remains protected until 2027 in countries applying a seventy-year term from the author’s death, and the United States position for a 1944 foreign photograph depends on restoration under the URAA. The photograph is dated 1944, well after the 1931 film documented here.
