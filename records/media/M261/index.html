Public domain in Israel: the Wikimedia Commons file carries the PD-Israel tag, under which copyright in a photograph made before 2008 expires fifty years after it was taken, so the term ended in 1994. That ground is Israeli, not United States law; Lerski died in 1956, so the work remains protected until 2027 in countries applying a seventy-year term from the author's death, and the United States position for a 1944 foreign photograph depends on restoration under the URAA.
