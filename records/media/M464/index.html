Staszic school report for 1915/16 — Przegląd Techniczny, 1917

Fit to window

The school report states that the 1915/16 year began on 6 September 1915. It does not name Kaper or date his admission.

About this item

Complete page 187 of the 1917 issue of Przegląd Techniczny containing the Staszic school’s report. It dates the start of the 1915/16 school year to 6 September and records the intake to each class; it does not name Kaper or establish his individual admission date.
