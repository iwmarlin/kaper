About this item

Portrait photograph of Ninon Steinhoff (captioned “Sra. Ninon Steinhof”) published with a notice announcing her lecture on Paul Valéry and Paul Claudel at the Lycée Français in Rio de Janeiro.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Diário de Notícias (Rio de Janeiro), ano I, no. 36, 17 July 1930, p. 15, article “Valéry e Claudel. Uma conferência no ‘Lycée Français’.”

Public domain. The photograph was published in Brazil in 1930 and no photographer is credited. Under Brazilian Law No. 9,610/1998, art. 44, copyright in photographic works lasts 70 years from 1 January of the year following first publication; that term has expired. In the United States, works published before 1 January 1931 are in the public domain. The local file is a reduced-resolution crop from the digitized newspaper page.
