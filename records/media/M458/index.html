Dajos Béla, photographed by Alexander Binder before 1929. His orchestra is documented in three recordings of songs by Kaper.

About this item

Studio portrait of the violinist and bandleader Dajos Béla, photographed by Alexander Binder before 1929 and preserved as a picture postcard in the collection of Universität Osnabrück.

Credit: Alexander Binder; picture-postcard collection, Universität Osnabrück, via Wikimedia Commons.
