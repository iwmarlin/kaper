United States–Mexico border crossing at Calexico, c. 1927

Fit to window

The Calexico–Mexicali crossing photographed for the Los Angeles Times about 1927. Kaper entered the United States here on 1 November 1935, eight years after this view was taken, so the picture shows the place rather than the day.
