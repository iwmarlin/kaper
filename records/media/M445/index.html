About this item

Press photograph published in the Santa Ana Journal on 27 January 1938 under the heading “Song Writers to Perform”: Lew Pollack and Sidney Mitchell seated over a sheet of music before appearing at the President’s Ball. The printed caption is reproduced beneath the picture.
