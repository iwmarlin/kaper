Hätt’ ich einen Kammerdiener? — manuscript title page, Berlin, 1926

Fit to window

Title page of Hätt’ ich einen Kammerdiener?, Berlin, 1926: words by Fritz Rotter, music by Rotter and “Dr. B. Kaper” — the earliest document that names the two together.

About this item

Detail of the title page of the manuscript, written on music paper: “Hätt’ ich einen Kammerdiener… Hätt’ ich eine Köchin…”, “Lied & Fox-trott”, “Text v. Fritz Rotter”, “Musik v. Fritz Rotter & Dr. B. Kaper”, “Berlin 1926”. The pages of music that follow are not reproduced.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Bronislaw Kaper Papers, collection 03193, Box 1, American Heritage Center, University of Wyoming; title page, detail.

Public domain as reproduced here: the detail shows only the title, a description of the genre, the authors’ names, a place and a date, none of which is a protected work, so nothing in it is protected in Poland, the European Union or the United States. The music on the following pages is not reproduced; it remains protected until the end of 2053. The research copy was supplied by the American Heritage Center, which leaves copyright clearance to the user.
