Allan Jones — publicity photograph, 1945

Fit to window

Allan Jones in a management photograph of April 1945. He introduced “Così, cosa” in A Night at the Opera and sang “Tomorrow is another day” and the outtake “A message from the man in the moon” in A Day at the Races.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Frederick Brothers Agency publicity photograph, 1945, via Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain. The photograph was published in the United States in 1945 without a copyright notice, which places it out of copyright there; the agency that issued it is named, but no photographer is credited.
