About this item

Jan Kiepura singing from a balcony to a crowd on Plac Piłsudskiego in Warsaw, photographed by Henryk Poddębski. Kiepura, the star of Ein Lied für Dich and Tout pour l’amour, kept the Kaper–Jurmann songs before European audiences after the composers left for America.
