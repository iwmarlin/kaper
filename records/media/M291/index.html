Summary

Edwin L. Marin, who directed Man of the People in 1937 and Everybody Sing in 1938, photographed with a script on the set of Paris Calling in 1941. The archive holds the detail cropped to Marin from the Universal publicity still, which also shows Elisabeth Bergner.
