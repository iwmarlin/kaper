Austin Egen — drawn portrait, c. 1930

Fit to window

Austin Egen in a drawing dated on the source page to about 1930. Egen composed and recorded with Kaper in Berlin; the two are credited together on “Ich weiß ein kleines Café” and “Diese Melodie sing ich nur für Sie”, both 1931.

Rights and provenance

Credit: German Wikipedia; the source page names no publication or holding institution.

Reuse rights are not cleared. The German-language Wikipedia file gives its source only as “Internet”, dates the drawing to about 1930 and claims expiry of the German, Austrian and Swiss terms because Egen died in 1941. That expiry took effect on 1 January 2012, after the 1996 URAA date, so United States copyright was restored and runs 95 years from publication; the file page itself carries a warning against transferring it to Wikimedia Commons without individual review. Because neither the publication nor its year is established, this reduced-resolution reproduction (301 × 567 px) is used for scholarly identification of a documented Kaper collaborator.
