“Von links nach rechts: Dr. Robert Katscher, Armin Robinson, Paul Whiteman, Direktor Erwin Hein, Fritz Wreede, Direktor Otto Hein” — the caption as printed in Die Bühne, no. 85 (1926): 12.

About this item

Photograph printed in the Viennese weekly Die Bühne in 1926, on a page of statements by Viennese authors published for Paul Whiteman’s visit to the city. It shows the American bandleader among Viennese musicians and publishers, the lyricist Armin Robinson among them; Robinson wrote the words of three songs Kaper composed in Berlin between 1930 and 1931.
