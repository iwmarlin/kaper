About this item

Reduced-resolution documentary image of the label of a Japanese Polydor pressing of “Song Is Ended. ” The label credits Hermann Bick and Dr. Kaper as a two-piano jazz duo and gives catalogue number 10237-B and matrix 1774 BK. The image supports the documented history of Kaper’s late-1920s jazz-piano partnership; it is not an audio asset.
