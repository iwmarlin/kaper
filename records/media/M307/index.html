Nicholas Brodszky — portrait in Délibáb, 1928

Fit to window

Nicholas Brodszky, printed as Bródszky Miklós, in Délibáb on 10 November 1928. The portrait accompanied an interview about his work for Budapest’s Városi Színház.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Délibáb, 2nd year, no. 45 (10 November 1928), p. 29; Electronic Periodicals Archive, National Széchényi Library.
