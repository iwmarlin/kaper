About this item

Listening reference for “Adieu, es ist zu schön, um wahr zu sein” in the recording by Bernhard Etté mit seinem Tanz-Orchester with Wolfgang Schmidt singing. The upload describes a Lindström recording of 1933 issued on Gloria in Germany and on Odeon abroad; the label is not shown.
