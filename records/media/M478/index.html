Fit to window

“Paris–Havre–New York”: the French Line boat train at the Le Havre quay, on a poster by Albert Sébille of about 1920–1930.

About this item

Colour lithograph poster by the marine painter Albert Sébille, issued jointly by the Chemins de fer de l’État and the Compagnie Générale Transatlantique to advertise the boat train from Paris to the liners at Le Havre. Passengers leave the train on the quay and board the ship under a canopy lettered “French Line”. Printed by Novia, Paris.
