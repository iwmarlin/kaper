    Heiratsregister (marriage register), Standesamt I in Berlin, P Rep. 800 Nr. 0284. Landesarchiv Berlin. Marriage of Bronisław Kaper (bride: surname Sirot), 30 June 1927. Standesamt I in Berlin was the German 'foreign registry office' for civil acts performed outside the Reich; its marriage registers for 1874–1929 are held at the Landesarchiv Berlin. Cropped entries reproduced as (Kaper) and (bride, Sirot).
