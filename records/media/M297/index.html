Summary

Herbert Stothart, who scored Mutiny on the Bounty and A Night at the Opera in 1935 and San Francisco in 1936, going over a new song with Ramon Novarro for Devil May Care. Detail of a photograph printed in Picture Play in 1930, in a feature on the theme song at the coming of sound.

Sources

  1. Picture Play Magazine, January–June 1930, p. 97, photograph of Herbert Stothart with Ramon Novarro
