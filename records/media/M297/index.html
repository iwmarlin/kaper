About this item

The printed caption reads: "Herbert Stothart, right, goes over a new song with Ramon Novarro, who sings several numbers in ‘Devil May Care. ’" The caption reverses the two men. The figure on the right is identifiable as Ramon Novarro by comparison with his published portraits, so the composer is the man on the left, in shirtsleeves with the sheet music, and it is that figure which is reproduced here. The caption names only these two, which is what makes the identification safe.
