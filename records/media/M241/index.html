  1. SRC0569 1936

    “Songs Played Over 10,000 Times.” Motion Picture Herald 124 (5 September 1936). American Society of Composers, Authors and Publishers tabulation of the 112 songs performed at least 10,000 times over the NBC and CBS networks during 1935–36. “You’re All I Need” ranks 14th with 21,424 broadcasts, listed under the source title Lie Like Gentlemen (Escapade) and credited to Bronislaw Kaper, Walter Jarmann [Jurmann] and Gus Kahn, published by Robbins.
