About this item

Sheet music for “Zgaś lampę! ,” piosenka-foxtrot, words by Willy, music by Br. Asper, described as the newest success of Perskie Oko. Published by Edition J. Altschuler, Warszawa–Riga, 1926. Plate no. J. A. 40. Polona / Biblioteka Narodowa.
