Jacques Monteux — SACEM membership photograph

Fit to window

Jacques Monteux, who wrote the French words of “En premières, en secondes, en troisièmes...” and “Sport” (1933) to music by Kaper and Jurmann. Identity photograph from his SACEM membership file, inscribed “Monteux Jacques Jacob”.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer and date unidentified; Document d’archives Sacem, Musée SACEM. © Fonds Sacem.

Reuse rights are not cleared. The holding museum states only “© Fonds Sacem” for the print, which measures 4.5 × 3.7 cm and carries no photographer, studio or date. The watermarked digital copy published by the museum is used for scholarly identification of a documented Kaper collaborator, with the holding institution and the unresolved attribution stated alongside it.
