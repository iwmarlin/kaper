Robert Wohlmuth and his crew at the Selenophon studio, 1930

Fit to window

The crew of Bünzlis Abenteuer in the Selenophon studio, as Mein Film printed them in 1930. The caption names the upper row: Bayer, Schnürch, Heinrich Bünzli, the director Robert Wohlmuth, Mizzi Gribl and Zehetmeier; and the second row: Perfall, Ledersteger, Wesely and Benda.

About this item

Production photograph from the Viennese Selenophon studio, printed in Mein Film in 1930, showing the director Robert Wohlmuth with the crew of the sound film Bünzlis Abenteuer. Wohlmuth directed Die Zwei vom Südexpreß two years later, for which Kaper and Jurmann wrote the songs.
