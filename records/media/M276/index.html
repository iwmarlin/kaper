Blüthner-Saal entrance, Lützowstraße 76, Berlin, 1912

Fit to window

Entrance to the Blüthner-Saal at Lützowstraße 76, Berlin, 1912. The venue was renamed Bach-Saal in 1927.

About this item

Historical view of the entrance and street façade of the Blüthner-Saal and Klindworth-Scharwenka-Saal complex at Lützowstraße 76, printed above a notice for Oskar Schwalm’s Piano-Magazin, the Blüthner piano salesroom in the same building. The Blüthner-Saal was renamed Bach-Saal in 1927.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; printed notice for Oskar Schwalm’s Piano-Magazin, place of first publication unidentified; reproduced on grammophon-platten.de.

Public-domain historical photograph: the printed image is dated to 1912 and no photographer is identified. The local file is a faithful, reduced-size reproduction used to document the venue.

  1. SRC0573 1912 (reported)

    Halftone photograph of the façade of Lützowstraße 76, Berlin, with the entrance to the Blüthner-Saal and Klindworth-Scharwenka-Saal, printed above a notice headed “Oskar Schwalm’s Piano-Magazin” announcing that the salesroom for Blüthner pianos had recently moved into the building. Reproduced by GrammophonTeam in the thread “Bachsaal und Schwechtensaal – Lützowstrasse” on grammophon-platten.de (post of 15 July 2016) and dated there to 1912. No photographer and no place of first publication are given.
