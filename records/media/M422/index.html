About this item

Listening copy of “C’est ton amour”, attributed to Tino Rossi by the Internet Archive collection and associated there with Columbia DF 1610. BnF independently catalogues Rossi with Marcel Cariven’s orchestra on that disc, dated [1934], but the transferred copy and take remain unverified.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Tino Rossi, as identified by the collection; The Popular Jazz Archive / Internet Archive.
