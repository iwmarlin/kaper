About this item

Sheet-music cover for “All God’s Chillun Got Rhythm,” published by Robbins Music Corporation in 1937 as a tie-in to the MGM film A Day at the Races. The object documents the song’s film association and commercial print circulation. The cover image is identified through That’s My Sheet Music, which attributes its images to the Richard Kraft collection; bibliographic publication data is independently supported by the U. S. Copyright Office Catalog of Copyright Entries.
