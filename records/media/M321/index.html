Rights and provenance

Credit: MNF Fotótár / National Film Institute Hungary; portrait detail from ‘Székely István és Haraszti Mici a Hyppolit, a lakáj forgatásakor,’ 1931.

The National Film Institute page credits MNF Fotótár and identifies István Székely and Mici Haraszti during production of Hyppolit, a lakáj in 1931, but names no photographer and provides no open reuse licence. The local 500 × 625 pixel file is an unretouched crop used at limited resolution for scholarly identification of a documented Kaper collaborator. The complete source photograph remains available through the linked institutional record.
