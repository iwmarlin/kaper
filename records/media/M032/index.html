About this gallery

Gallery of press items, posters and contextual materials relating to Bronisław Kaper’s Paris years and his work in French cinema and theatrical culture, 1933–1934.

Gallery · 6 images

Abenteuer am Lido — advertisement in Mein Film, 14 September 1933

Fit to window

Advertisement for Abenteuer am Lido, published in Mein Film on 14 September 1933, with music credited to Kaper and Jurmann.

L’Informateur de Seine, 30 March 1934 — advertisement for Mariage à responsabilité limitée

Fit to window

Advertisement for Mariage à responsabilité limitée, the French-language version of Ehe mit beschränkter Haftung, naming Florelle and crediting the music to Kaper and Jurmann. L’Informateur de Seine, 30 March 1934.

Une femme au volant — La Critique cinématographique, 8 July 1933

Fit to window

Production announcement for Une femme au volant, published in La Critique cinématographique on 8 July 1933, crediting the music to Walter Jurmann and Bronisław Kaper.

Le Matin, 14 December 1934 — Jacques Ibert on the music of Les Nuits moscovites

Fit to window

“La musique des Nuits moscovites,” Jacques Ibert’s review of the music of Les Nuits moscovites (1934), with a scene still, in Le Matin, 14 December 1934. Ibert praises the score and its use in two scenes played by Harry Baur, but names neither composer; Kaper and Jurmann wrote the film’s songs and had left for Hollywood by the time the review appeared.

Jan Kiepura reception in Paris, with Bronisław and Eleonora Kaper — photograph, November 1933

Fit to window

Bronisław and Eleonora Kaper among guests at a reception for Jan Kiepura in Paris, November 1933.

Hôtel Le Château Frontenac, 54 rue Pierre Charron, Paris — advertisement, 1935

Fit to window

Advertisement for the Hôtel Le Château Frontenac, 54 rue Pierre Charron, Paris 8e. Le Sport universel illustré, 15 October 1935.
