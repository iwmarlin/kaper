The World on Mercator’s Projection — Edward Stanford Ltd., 1926

Fit to window

Edward Stanford’s The World on Mercator’s Projection, 1926, from Stanford’s London Atlas of Universal Geography. It serves as the historical layer of the map explorer below zoom level five, trimmed of its atlas margins.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Edward Stanford Ltd., The World on Mercator’s Projection, 1926; David Rumsey Map Collection, David Rumsey Map Center, Stanford University Libraries.

Public domain. The map was published in London in 1926, before 1 January 1931, so United States copyright has expired, and the same sheet is held on Wikimedia Commons as a public-domain file. The scan comes from the David Rumsey Map Collection, list number 14508.007, credited here as the digitising institution. The reproduction measures 2400 × 1724 px, trimmed of the atlas margins without alteration to the cartographic content.
