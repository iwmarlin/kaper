Erst- und Neuaufführungen, 30 November–3 December 1927 — Führer durch die Konzertsäle Berlins

Fit to window

“Erst- und Neuaufführungen in der Zeit vom 30. November bis 3. Dezember 1927”: the last line enters Kaper's Klavierkonzert at the Bachsaal on 3 December.

About this item

The Berlin concert guide's table of first and new performances for 30 November to 3 December 1927, five entries in all. The last reads “3. Dezember — Bachsaal — B. Kaper: Klavierkonzert”, where Klavierkonzert names the kind of concert rather than a title: the line above enters “Kodaly: Klavierkonzert”, and Kodály wrote no piano concerto. The guide lists no other concert at the Bachsaal that day, and the evening is Róża Etkin's recital, advertised in the same issue with Kaper's work marked as an Uraufführung.
