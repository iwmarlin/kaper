Richard Tauber, who recorded “Schade, daß Liebe ein Märchen ist” by Kaper and Jurmann in 1932, photographed by the Bain News Service, c. 1920–1925.

About this item

Portrait of tenor Richard Tauber, photographed between approximately 1920 and 1925. Bain News Service negative; George Grantham Bain Collection, Library of Congress Prints and Photographs Division. LCCN 2014714361; digital ID ggbain. 34209.
