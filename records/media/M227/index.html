About this item

Opening folios of Bronisław Kaper’s student record book (“Wykaz wykładów i ćwiczeń”) at the University of Warsaw, Faculty of Law and Political Sciences. The spread includes his student portrait and matriculation information. University of Warsaw Archives, Bronisław Kaper student file, RP 6854, fols. 38–39.
