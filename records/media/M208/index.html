Variety, 27 November 1935: the Bronislaw Kaper–Walter Jurmann tune “Music on the Starlit Water” has been set as the theme for Metro’s Last of the Pagans, with lyrics by Ned Washington.

About this item

Press clipping from Variety (New York), 27 November 1935, reporting that the Kaper–Jurmann tune “Music on the Starlit Water,” with lyrics by Ned Washington, was set as the theme for Last of the Pagans. Digitized by Internet Archive / Media History Digital Library.
