Kurjer Warszawski, 27 March 1929 — Harmonja concert conducted by Kaper

Fit to window

The paragraph of “Kolonja polska w Berlinie” (Kurjer Warszawski, 27 March 1929) reporting a concert of the Berlin “Harmonja” conducted by “the young composer dr. Bronisław Kaper,” with the tenor Salecki of the Dresden Opera and the soprano Głowacka.
