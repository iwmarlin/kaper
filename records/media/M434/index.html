Jean de Limur — portrait, Comœdia, 12 December 1924

Fit to window

Jean de Limur, who directed Mariage à responsabilité limitée (1933), with songs by Kaper and Jurmann, in a portrait published in Comœdia on 12 December 1924.

About this item

Press portrait of the director Jean de Limur, printed in the Paris daily Comœdia on 12 December 1924, nine years before he directed the French film for which Kaper wrote.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Comœdia, 12 December 1924, p. 4. RetroNews, Bibliothèque nationale de France.

Public domain. The photograph is printed without a photographer’s or agency’s credit and is therefore treated as an anonymous work published in 1924, for which the seventy-year term of protection in France ran out at the end of 1994.
