Pete Wendling, at the right, with Jimmy Monaco, in a photograph connected with the Pathé film Words and Music, published in The Talking Machine World in October 1927.

About this item

Publicity photograph of the songwriters Jimmy Monaco, at the left, and Pete Wendling, at the right, made in connection with the Pathé film Words and Music and printed in the American trade paper The Talking Machine World in October 1927.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; The Talking Machine World 23, no. 10 (October 1927): 137. World Radio History archive.

Public domain. The photograph was published in an American trade paper in October 1927, before the threshold of 1930 from which copyright still runs in the United States; no photographer or agency is credited beside it.
