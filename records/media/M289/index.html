Edmund Goulding — Photoplay, December 1922

Fit to window

Edmund Goulding, who directed Maytime in 1937. The portrait is much earlier: it was printed in Photoplay in December 1922, when Goulding was working in Hollywood as a screenwriter.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Photoplay 22 (December 1922), p. 61, digitised at the Internet Archive; Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain in the United States. The photograph was first published in Photoplay in December 1922, before 1 January 1931, so United States copyright has expired. The Wikimedia Commons file page notes that the image may still be protected outside the United States in jurisdictions that do not apply the rule of the shorter term to United States works. The photographer is not named in the magazine.
