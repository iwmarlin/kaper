About this item

Identification follows the archival caption, which names the guests: Jan Kiepura fifth from the right, the Paris photographer Zygmunt Frenkiel first from the left, Bronisław Kaper second from the left, the director Ryszard Ordyński fifth from the left, the singer Adam Didur ninth from the right, the poet Jan Lechoń in the centre in spectacles, Kaper's wife in the centre, and — among the women at the right edge — Kiepura's secretary Marcella Halicz together with Alicja Brun, whom the caption distinguishes as the one holding a photograph of Kiepura. The woman shown in this detail stands beside the guest holding that photograph and is therefore Halicz. The detail was located in the full frame by correlation: it corresponds to a 315 × 466 px region of the 2035 × 2560 px scan and is reproduced here enlarged.
