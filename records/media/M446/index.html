Fit to window

Leonard Whitcup, co-composer of “Fiesta (Bianca)” (1932), which Kaper arranged, in the portrait printed with the notice of his appointment as managing director of Music, Music, Music, Inc. — Billboard, 14 May 1966.

About this item

Portrait of the songwriter Leonard Whitcup printed with the trade announcement of his appointment as managing director of the publishing house Music, Music, Music, Inc. , captioned simply “Whitcup”.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Billboard, 14 May 1966, p. 3. World Radio History archive.

Reuse rights are not cleared.
