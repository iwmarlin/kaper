About this item

Early twentieth-century postcard view of rue Alphonse-de-Neuville in the Plaine Monceau quarter of Paris. It provides period street context for the Polish Consulate General, located at number 19 in the late 1920s and 1930s; the postcard does not establish that the consulate building itself is visible.
