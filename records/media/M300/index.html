Public domain in the United States. Who's Who at Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer was issued in 1937 by the studio's publicity department as a reference book for the press. The digitised copy carries no copyright notice: none appears on the title page, on the leaves facing it, or in the end matter, and a United States work published in 1937 without notice entered the public domain on publication. Had a notice been present, the copyright would in any case have required renewal in 1964 or 1965. No photographer is credited for the individual portraits.
