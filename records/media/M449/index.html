About this item

Studio portrait in profile of the singer and actor Luigi Bernauer, reproduced beside the entry on his Homocord record of 1930 with the Fred Bird Rhythmicans. A small emblem of a radio tower above a low hall is printed in the upper left corner; its lettering cannot be read at this size.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer uncredited; picture archives of Jens-Uwe Völmecke and Richard Weize, reproduced in Dreh’n wir mal das Radio an (…and more bears, 2024), p. 62.

Reuse rights are not cleared. The publication that reproduces the portrait names no photographer, giving only the private picture archives from which its illustrations come, and the edition itself was issued in 2024 with rights reserved. The local 568 × 793 pixel reproduction is used for scholarly identification of a documented Kaper performer, with the publication, the picture archives and the unresolved attribution stated alongside it.
