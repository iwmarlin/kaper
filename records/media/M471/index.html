Grand-Montrouge, Grande Rue, on a postcard of the early twentieth century — the suburb of Paris where Salabert had its recording studio.

About this item

Picture postcard of the Grande Rue at the Avenue de la République in Grand-Montrouge, posted in Montrouge in the early twentieth century. It shows the suburb south of Paris where Salabert had its recording studio; the studio itself is not depicted.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Postcard, photographer unidentified; Archives départementales des Hauts-de-Seine, 9FI/MON_17; via Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain. The postcard was published without a photographer’s credit and posted well before 1931, so the French term for an anonymous work has expired and the card is out of copyright in the United States by date. The Archives départementales des Hauts-de-Seine publish the digitisation under the Licence Ouverte, which asks for attribution of the source.
