William Axt — portrait photograph, 1920s

Fit to window

William Axt, who composed the score of The Perfect Gentleman (1935) and was music director of Everybody Sing (1938), both films with songs by Kaper and Jurmann, in a portrait dated to the 1920s by the source file page.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; image dated to the 1920s; Russian Wikipedia, File:William Axt.jpg; upstream source credited there to Monstrous Movie Music.

Russian Wikipedia labels the file public domain, but its metadata does not identify the photographer or document the original publication, so that status cannot be independently confirmed. The unchanged 117 × 158 pixel source-resolution image is used for scholarly identification of a documented Kaper collaborator, with attribution and links to the available provenance.
