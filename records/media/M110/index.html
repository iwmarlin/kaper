Rights and provenance

Credit: Filmposter-Archiv, Madame wünscht keine Kinder (Austria, 1933), design by Anton Ziegler; reuse rights not cleared.

Filmposter-Archiv identifies Anton Ziegler as the designer of this 1933 Austrian poster but grants no reuse licence. Because the work has an identified creator, it cannot be treated as an expired anonymous publication; the local reproduction is classified as restricted.
