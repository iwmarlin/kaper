Joseph Santley — Celebrated Actor Folks’ Cookeries, 1916

Fit to window

Joseph Santley, who directed We Went to College in 1936. The portrait is much earlier and shows him as a stage actor: it was printed in Celebrated Actor Folks’ Cookeries in 1916, two decades before he began directing in Hollywood.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Celebrated Actor Folks’ Cookeries (New York: Mabel Rowland, Inc., 1916), p. 77; Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain in the United States. The portrait was published in a United States book of 1916, before 1 January 1931, so United States copyright has expired. The book credits no photographer — the Wikimedia Commons author field names the publisher, Mabel Rowland, Inc. — so no term can be computed from a photographer’s death, and the image may remain protected in jurisdictions that do not apply the rule of the shorter term to United States works.
