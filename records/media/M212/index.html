Das Kino-Journal, 20 December 1930 — announcement of the new Richard Tauber film

Fit to window

Das Kino-Journal, 20 December 1930, announcing the next Richard Tauber sound film for Emelka, directed by Max Reichmann, with Marianne Winkelstern, Margo Lion and Siegfried Arno: Paul Dessau leads the music, Fritz Rotter writes the song lyrics and “Dr. Kaper” the music of the hits; shooting to begin on 2 January at Tempelhof, with Tauber as a singing conductor. The film was released in 1931 as Die große Attraktion.
