About this item

Pen caricature of Franz Wenzler in profile, wearing a driving cap and a thick rolled scarf, signed by the draughtsman. It appeared on a page of sketches from the Semmering motor race, captioned simply “Direktor Franz Wenzler. ”

Rights and provenance

Credit: Drawing by Hans Schaukal; Die Bühne, no. 203 (1928), p. 20. ANNO – Austrian Newspapers Online, Österreichische Nationalbibliothek.
