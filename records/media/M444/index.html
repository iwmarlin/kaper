Sidney Clare with Hoagy Carmichael at the Beachcomber — Desert Sun, 1956

Fit to window

Sidney Clare (left), co-composer of “Miss Annabelle Lee”, which Kaper arranged as a two-piano novelty and recorded with Hermann Bick, with Hoagy Carmichael (centre) and Arthur Gordon at the Beachcomber. Printed in the Desert Sun on 13 October 1956.

About this item

Press photograph from the opening night of the Beachcomber in Palm Springs, published in the Desert Sun on 13 October 1956: Sidney Clare at the left, Hoagy Carmichael in the centre and Arthur Gordon of Fleischmann Distilleries at the right. The printed caption is reproduced beneath the picture.
