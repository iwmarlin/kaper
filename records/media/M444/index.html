About this item

Press photograph from the opening night of the Beachcomber in Palm Springs, published in the Desert Sun on 13 October 1956: Sidney Clare at the left, Hoagy Carmichael in the centre and Arthur Gordon of Fleischmann Distilleries at the right. The printed caption is reproduced beneath the picture.
