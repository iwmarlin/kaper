Hotel Kaiserhof, Berlin, 1932 — where Goebbels addressed the film industry on 28 March 1933.

About this item

The entrance of the Hotel Kaiserhof on Wilhelmplatz in 1932. On 28 March 1933 Joseph Goebbels addressed the film industry here; the next day the Ufa board resolved to dismiss its Jewish staff.
