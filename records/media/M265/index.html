René Pujol — portrait by Henri Manuel, 1932

Fit to window

René Pujol, the Bordeaux-born librettist who wrote with Charles-Louis Pothier under Albert Willemetz at the Bouffes-Parisiens and supplied the words of “Ni mal, ni bien” and “Un regard de femme” (1934) to music by Kaper and Jurmann, photographed by Henri Manuel for the programme of La Pouponnière.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Henri Manuel (1874–1947); programme of La Pouponnière, Bouffes-Parisiens, 1932, collection Jacques Gana, via the Encyclopédie de la comédie musicale.

Reuse rights are not cleared. The photographer, Henri Manuel, died in 1947, so the French term expired on 1 January 2018; because the work was still protected in France on the URAA date of 1 January 1996, United States copyright was restored and runs ninety-five years from the 1932 publication, to 1 January 2028.
