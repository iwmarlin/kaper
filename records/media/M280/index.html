Staszic School, Warsaw — architectural view, 1916

Architectural view of the Staszic School at historical Polna 60, now Noakowskiego 6, Warsaw, published in 1916 after plans by Marian Kontkiewicz and Stanisław Zaleski.

About this item

Printed architectural view of the purpose-built Staszic School at historical Polna 60 (now Noakowskiego 6), published in April 1916 while the building was still being completed. The original caption identifies the design as the work of architects Marian Kontkiewicz and Stanisław Zaleski.
