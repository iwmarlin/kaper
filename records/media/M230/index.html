Fit to window

Koszutski Girls in a 1926 illustrated programme of Warsaw’s Teatr “Perskie Oko.”

About this item

Page 8 of the illustrated programme Teatr “Perskie Oko”: przegląd rewji (Warsaw, 1926), with three ensemble photographs labeled “Koszutski-Girls. ” The image provides visual context for the troupe named in Kaper’s 1923 manuscript; it does not document the manuscript’s authorship or the “Mus. B. Asper” inscription.
