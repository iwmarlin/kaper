About this item

Press photograph of the director Jean Choux crouching beside the camera during a take, published with the interview series “Les metteurs en scène devant l’évolution du cinéma”.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer uncredited; Excelsior, 25 December 1936, p. 4. RetroNews, Bibliothèque nationale de France.

Public domain. The photograph is printed without a photographer’s or agency’s credit and is therefore treated as an anonymous work published in 1936, for which the seventy-year term of protection in France ran out at the end of 2006.
