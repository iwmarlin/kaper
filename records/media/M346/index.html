Hans J. Salter, 1940s — Cinefantastique, 1978

Fit to window

Hans J. Salter in the portrait printed with Preston Neal Jones’s interview “The Ghost of Hans J. Salter” in Cinefantastique in 1978, where it is captioned “Hans J. Salter circa 1940s.” No photographer is named. Salter worked with Kaper on three films of 1933 before both reached Hollywood.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Cinefantastique 7, no. 2 (Summer 1978), p. 10; digitized copy at Internet Archive.
