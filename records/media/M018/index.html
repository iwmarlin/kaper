About this item

UFA musical comedy directed by Kurt Gerron, featuring songs by Bronisław Kaper and Walter Jurmann, including "Heut' bin ich gut aufgelegt". The film's opening (first 5 minutes) is available on filmportal. de, digitized in 2023 as part of the Förderprogramm Filmerbe (FFE). Click above to watch on the original source.
