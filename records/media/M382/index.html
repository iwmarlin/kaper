Siegfried Arno in Schatz, mach’ Kasse, 1926

Fit to window

Siegfried Arno in the Ama-Film comedy Schatz, mach’ Kasse (1926), in a photograph printed in the magazine Das Leben and cropped from a scene still with Ossi Oswalda. Five years later he sang the refrain of Kaper’s “Ach, Otto, Otto...!” with Marek Weber’s orchestra for Electrola.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Das Leben 4, no. 7 (January 1926/27), via Wikimedia Commons.

Public domain. The photograph carries no photographer’s credit and was published in 1926, more than seventy years ago, which places an anonymous work out of copyright in the European Union; publication before 1 January 1931 places it out of copyright in the United States.
