‘Le Canard aux Navets’ luncheon group photograph, 1922

Fit to window

Guests at the ‘Canard aux Navets’ luncheon in Paris, 1922. The printed caption names Jean Legrand as the third person listed under ‘Tout en haut’; the full photograph is retained as documentary context rather than used as an individual portrait.

About this item

Group photograph from the ‘Canard aux Navets’ luncheon, published in Comœdia on 15 March 1922 together with a row-by-row caption. The caption includes ‘Jean Legrand’ as the third name under ‘Tout en haut’, and the accompanying report separately lists ‘André et Jean Legrand’ among the guests. The complete photograph and its printed caption are retained as documentary evidence; the individual figure is too small to serve as a reliable portrait.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Comœdia, 15 March 1922, p. 2. RetroNews, Bibliothèque nationale de France.

Public domain. The photograph was published in France in 1922 without a photographer or agency credit and is treated as an anonymous work whose seventy-year French term expired at the end of 1992; publication before 1931 also places it in the public domain in the United States.
