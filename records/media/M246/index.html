Henri Varna — portrait plate from the recueil “Féerie de Paris”, 1937

Fit to window

Henri Varna, captioned on the plate as director of the Casino de Paris and the Alcazar de Paris. From a Bibliothèque nationale de France album of press cuttings and programmes for his revue “Féerie de Paris”, covering the performance of 20 December 1937. Varna wrote the French words for four Jurmann–Kaper songs in the 1934 Casino de Paris revue Parade de France.
