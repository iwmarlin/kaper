Robert Wohlmuth — portrait, 1930

Robert Wohlmuth in 1930, at work on Bünzlis Abenteuer: a detail of the studio photograph printed in Mein Film, no. 248, page 7.

About this item

Detail of the Selenophon studio photograph printed in Mein Film in 1930, showing the director Robert Wohlmuth. The caption names the upper row in order and places Mizzi Gribl, the only woman in it, fifth; Wohlmuth stands immediately to her left.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Photographer unidentified; Mein Film, no. 248 (1930): 7, detail. Österreichische Nationalbibliothek / ANNO.

Public domain. Detail of a photograph published in an Austrian film weekly in 1930 with no photographer credited; the Austrian term for a simple photograph expired fifty years after publication.
