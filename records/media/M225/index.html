Kind, ich freu’ mich auf Dein Kommen (1933) — promotional sheet-music cover

Fit to window

Cover of the Ufaton-Verlag song edition for Kind, ich freu’ mich auf Dein Kommen (1933), “ein Ufaton-Film, Herstellung: Günther Stapenhorst”: texts by Fritz Rotter, music by Walter Jurmann and B. Kaper. Drawing signed Herzig.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Walter Jurmann official cover gallery.

The Walter Jurmann cover gallery documents this German promotional cover but supplies no open reuse licence. A graphic work published in Germany in 1933 remained protected on 1 January 1996, so United States copyright was restored under the URAA. The local image is classified as restricted.
