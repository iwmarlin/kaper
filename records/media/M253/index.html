Georg Jacoby on location in the Netherlands, 1953

Fit to window

Georg Jacoby during the filming of Die geschiedene Frau in the Dutch bulb-growing region, 26 April 1953 — two decades after the work documented here. Jacoby directed Melodie der Liebe (1932), for which Kaper wrote music.
