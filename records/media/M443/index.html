About this item

Cabinet card portrait of Tchaikovsky taken in 1888 and attributed to the Paris photographer Émile Reutlinger, cropped to the figure from the full card.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Attributed to Émile Reutlinger; New York Public Library / Wikimedia Commons, public domain.

Public domain. The photograph is attributed to Émile Reutlinger, who died in 1907, so the term of the author’s life and seventy years has long expired; it was also published in 1904, before the United States threshold of 1930. Attribution to the photographer and to the holding collection is retained.
