Moscow Nights (1935) — film poster

Fit to window

Poster for Moscow Nights (1935) at the Leicester Square Theatre, London: “C. M. Woolf presents” Harry Baur, Laurence Olivier and Penelope Dudley Ward, printed as a Denham production produced by Alexis Granowsky and directed by Anthony Asquith. The AFI Catalog credits London Film Productions as production company.
