About this gallery

Covers of early Polish sheet-music editions of Kaper’s songs published under the Br. Asper pseudonym: “A zresztą niech pan do mnie zadzwoni...” and “Zgaś lampę!”, both Edition J. Altschuler, 1926.

Gallery · 2 images

A zresztą niech pan do mnie zadzwoni... — sheet music, Polona

Fit to window

Sheet music for the foxtrot “A zresztą niech pan do mnie zadzwoni...,” with words by Willy and music by Br. Asper, from Zula Pogorzelska’s repertoire at Perskie Oko. Warsaw–Riga: Edition J. Altschuler, 1926.

Zgaś lampę! — sheet music, Polona

Fit to window

Sheet music for the song-foxtrot “Zgaś lampę!,” with words by Willy and music by Br. Asper, advertised as the newest success of the Perskie Oko theatre. Warsaw–Riga: Edition J. Altschuler, 1926.
