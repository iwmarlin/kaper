Charles-Louis Pothier — portrait, Flossie programme, 1929

Fit to window

Charles-Louis Pothier, the Bordeaux-born lyricist who worked with René Pujol as librettist-parolier to Albert Willemetz at the Bouffes-Parisiens and wrote the French words of “Ninon, quand tu me souris” and “O Madona!” (1933) to music by Kaper and Jurmann, in the author’s portrait from the programme of Flossie.

Rights and provenance

Credit: Pierre Carles; programme of Flossie, Bouffes-Parisiens, 1929, collection Jacques Gana, via the Encyclopédie de la comédie musicale.
