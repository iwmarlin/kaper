## SRC0313 / M060 / M061: Berlin marriage name index

The full 37-page PDF was downloaded from Landesarchiv Berlin. Its first page
reads P Rep. 800, Standesamt Berlin I, Nr. 284, Namensverzeichnis Heiratsregister
1927. Rendered PDF p. 15 contains the Kaper entry and p. 28 the Sirot entry;
both supply 30 June and register number 273, matching the retained crops.
This is the alphabetical name index, not a reproduction of the complete act.

The official registry holdings list (StandesaemterBerlin_Bestaende.pdf, dated
3 July 2023) identifies P Rep. 800 as Berlin I, the former district registry
for Mitte. The previous claim that this witness came from the separate
foreign-registry function of Standesamt I in Berlin was incorrect and is
removed, not merely moved into a note. The repository link is retained and
supplemented with the official holdings overview and precise PDF locators.

The structured 30 June 1927 is now `described_item`: the marriage indexed,
not a separately established compilation date for the alphabetical index.
M060 and M061 are described as cropped index entries. Their assets, rights
status and graph relationships remain unchanged.
