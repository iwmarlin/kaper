# People audit, 20 November 2026

The 188 Person records were checked in three ways:

- the 94 GND identifiers, each resolved through lobid.org and compared with
  the record's name and life dates;
- every person credit marked `confirmed`, for whether it rests on a
  document of the time or only on the later compilations: Sieghardt's
  filmography, the Walter Jurmann works catalogue, IMDb and Görner;
- the records themselves, for duplicates, roles and links.

## GND

All 94 identifiers resolve to the person named.

- **Life dates.** GND agrees with the record's dates except in four cases.
  All four were already `disputed`, with a note setting out the conflict:
  - `P003` Andrzej Włast;
  - `P065` René Pujol;
  - `P133` Eddy Duchin;
  - `P139` Jeanette MacDonald.
- **Two records without dates.** `P213` Michele Galdieri and `P214`
  Alfredo Bracchi had no dates. They now take GND's: 1902–1965 and
  1897–1976.

### Lowered to `probable`

These credits rest on the later compilations alone.

| Credit | Why |
|---|---|
| Kaper, the four *Chant du destin* songs (`W-S088`–`W-S091`) | The Coda edition of 1934 credits the music to Pierre Candel, Jurmann’s pseudonym, alone |
| Kaper, Jurmann, Roubier and Poterat, “À Paris tiguidiguidi” (`W-S097`) | The BnF record of the 1934 disc names Kaper as conductor only. The work stays `confirmed` on that documented part |
| Roubier, “Pourvu qu’on ait vingt ans” (`W-S116`) | The Salabert disc names only Kaper and Jurmann as composers |
| Kaper, Jurmann and Féline, “J’ai un message pour toi” (`W-S177`) | Besides Sieghardt there is only the metadata of a 2014 digital reissue. The song, its work and two relations are now `probable`, with the archive’s standard sentence |
| Jurmann, *Moscow Nights*, *Two Hearts in Wax Time* and *Un coup de rouge*; Roudès as director of *Un coup de rouge* | The films themselves are `probable` |

### Checked and kept

Garat’s performances and Pujol’s words for *On a volé un homme* rest on the
sheet-music cover of 1934. The cover is reproduced in the Jurmann
society’s gallery (`SRC0540`). It is a document of the time, so the credits
stay `confirmed`.

## Records

- **Duplicates.** There are none.
- **Names.** Display names and authorized headings differ only where the
  heading is a later form or a pseudonym. Examples are Steve Sekely for
  István Székely and Ben Berlin for Hermann Biek.
- **Roles.** `P130` Hermann Biek lacked the role of conductor, which his
  credits require. It is added.
- **People without credits.** Ten people have no credits, among them
  Louis B. Mayer, Ernst Lubitsch, Eleonora Kaper and Carl Laemmle. Each is
  linked to the Timeline Events in which they appear.

## Not checked

Library of Congress and VIAF headings were not re-read, because those
sites refuse automated access.
