Editorial method

Evidence before completeness

This archive distinguishes confirmed, probable and uncertain attributions. It preserves historical name forms while resolving people and organizations to stable public identities.

Scope note. The present public dataset documents Kaper's life and work through 1939. It is not a complete account of his later Hollywood career.
