Editorial method

Evidence before completeness

This archive distinguishes documented evidence from qualified attribution. It preserves historical name forms while resolving people and organizations to stable public identities.

Scope note. The present public dataset documents Kaper's life and work through 1939. It is not a complete account of his later Hollywood career.
